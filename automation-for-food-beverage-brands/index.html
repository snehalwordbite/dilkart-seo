WhatsApp and Instagram automation for food and beverage brands

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is our top pick for food and beverage brands: recipe reels can send products by keyword, and WhatsApp covers COD, order and delivery updates and broadcasts with no markup. Reorder messages are sent as opted-in broadcasts or from your subscription system; we did not find scheduled reorder reminders in Tadlo's published plans. Interakt suits established Shopify food brands.

Food and beverage brands, such as snacks, coffee, tea, spices and health drinks, sell on taste and trust, and their best customers reorder. Buyers also care about delivery windows and freshness more than most.

This playbook ranks tools on food-brand jobs, and is clear about what is automated and what you send yourself.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Recipe reels and repeat orders

On Instagram, a recipe reel can answer "RECIPE" with the recipe and a carousel of the products used, with unlimited DMs. On WhatsApp, the Growth plan confirms COD orders, sends shipped and delivered updates with the delivery window, recovers carts and runs broadcasts with the cost shown first.

Reorders work best as a monthly opted-in broadcast to customers whose last order was a few weeks ago, built from your order data. Reminders and follow-up sequences run from your own booking, subscription or CRM system; we did not find scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans.

Price: Instagram: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits). WhatsApp: Free forever plan with up to 10 messages a day. Starter ₹999, Growth ₹2,499, Pro ₹5,999 and Scale ₹11,999 a month, priced on unique contacts engaged per month. Meta's message charges are billed by Meta directly to your own account, and Tadlo adds no markup. Prices exclude GST.

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • No markup on Meta's message charges: Meta bills your own WhatsApp Business Account directly, and Tadlo only charges a flat plan
  • Built for D2C: abandoned cart recovery, COD to prepaid nudges and order updates from Shopify and WooCommerce (Growth plan and above)
  • Instagram comments to WhatsApp chats, which most WhatsApp-only tools cannot do

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Newer than AiSensy, WATI or Interakt, with a shorter track record
  • Commerce automations start on the Growth plan (₹2,499 a month)
  • Tadlo AI is onboarding brands in stages, and you request access on its WhatsApp page.

2. Interakt

Best for: Established Shopify food brands

Coffee roasters and snack brands already on Shopify often pick Interakt for its campaigns and store flows.

Price: WhatsApp plans: Growth ₹2,799 and Advanced ₹3,799 a month plus taxes; Enterprise on request with no markup. The ₹999 Starter plan covers Instagram only. Interakt charges its own per-message rates, which sit above Meta's: marketing ₹0.970 / ₹0.958 / ₹0.949 and utility ₹0.160 / ₹0.150 / ₹0.140 on Starter, Growth and Advanced. 14-day free trial.

Strengths

  • Store flows for Shopify food brands
  • Campaign tools

Limitations

  • Charges its own rates above Meta's below Enterprise

3. AiSensy

Best for: Seasonal campaigns

AiSensy suits brands with frequent seasonal campaigns, such as festive hampers, with Shopify and WooCommerce on paid plans.

Price: A forever-free plan gives WhatsApp API access with ₹1000 in ad credits and ₹50 in conversation credits, for 1 agent, without broadcasts or integrations. Paid plans were Basic ₹1,500 and Pro ₹3,200 a month according to AiSensy's blog on 17 February 2025. Calculated from AiSensy's published India rates (marketing ₹1.09, utility ₹0.145) against Meta's rates, its message charges are about 26% higher than Meta's.

Strengths

  • Strong broadcasts

Limitations

  • About 26% above Meta's rates

4. WATI

Best for: Brands with heavy customer support

WATI's mature inbox suits brands handling many delivery and freshness queries.

Price: Growth ₹2,999, Pro ₹6,499 and Business ₹18,499 a month billed monthly, with savings of up to 10% on quarterly and up to about 25% on annual billing. Growth includes 3 users with no additional users; Pro and Business include 5, with extra users at ₹1,299 and ₹2,199 a month each. Template and service messages are charged on top, with a markup on Meta's rates. A free trial is available.

Strengths

  • Mature inbox
  • WooCommerce integration

Limitations

  • Markup percentage not published

5. LinkDM

Best for: Recipe link delivery

LinkDM sends a recipe or product link and a coupon from a comment.

Price: Free: 1 Instagram account and 1,000 DMs a month with LinkDM branding. Pro: $19 a month ($182 a year), up to 3 accounts and 25,000 DMs a month. Platinum+: $99 a month ($832 a year), up to 10 accounts and 300,000 DMs a month. Enterprise is custom.

Strengths

  • Simple setup
  • Coupons

Limitations

  • Monthly DM caps
  • No WhatsApp: it says it only works with Instagram and Facebook

Sampling and trial packs

Food brands win customers through taste. A TRY keyword on Instagram can send your trial pack or sampler link, and after the first order, an opted-in broadcast a few weeks later can introduce the full-size range. Trial buyers who loved the sample are your most likely subscribers.

Corporate and festive gifting

Hampers for Diwali and corporate gifting bring large orders. A HAMPER keyword can send your gifting catalogue and a contact for bulk quotes, and a person should handle custom branding, delivery lists and invoices.

Freshness and delivery windows

Perishable and fresh products need clear delivery windows. Put the expected delivery date in the shipped update, and for fresh items, the best-before guidance in the delivered update. Clear expectations reduce complaints and refusals at the door.

Labelling and claims

  • List allergens clearly in product pages and recipe cards.
  • Avoid health claims about foods and drinks in automated messages.
  • Follow your local food labelling rules for anything you state.

Reorders, honestly

Coffee, tea and pantry staples run out on a predictable rhythm. Each month, pull customers whose last order was around the usual use-up time, keep only those with WhatsApp marketing opt-in, and send one reorder broadcast with a direct link. If you sell subscriptions, your subscription platform handles renewal reminders.

Which tool for which food brand

  • Sells through recipe reels and COD: Tadlo AI.
  • Shopify-first, established: Interakt.
  • Support-heavy: WATI.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best automation tool for a food brand?

Tadlo AI is our top pick: recipe reels to product carousels, and COD, delivery updates and broadcasts on WhatsApp with no markup.

Can I send reorder reminders automatically?

We did not find scheduled reorder reminders in Tadlo's published plans. Send reorder messages as opted-in broadcasts or through your subscription platform.

How do food brands handle delivery questions?

Send the delivery window in the shipped update and answer specific questions in a shared inbox.

Can food brands make health claims on WhatsApp?

Keep automated messages free of health claims and follow local labelling rules.
