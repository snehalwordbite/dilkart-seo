WhatsApp welcome message templates for online stores

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Below are 8 WhatsApp welcome message templates for online stores, for new subscribers, first-time chats, away hours and returning customers, with variables like {{name}}. A good welcome confirms what the customer signed up for and offers one next step. We recommend Tadlo AI for sending them, with automated replies from its Starter plan.

The first WhatsApp message a customer gets from your store sets the tone for every one after it. It should confirm who you are, what they will receive, and how to stop.

Some of these are templates you submit to Meta, such as a subscription welcome; others are automated replies inside a conversation the customer started.

For new subscribers

Template 1: opt-in confirmed

Hi {{name}}, welcome to {{store}} on WhatsApp! You'll get order updates and {{frequency}} offers here. Reply STOP any time to opt out of offers.

Template 2: restock list

Hi {{name}}, you're on the restock list for {{product_name}}. We'll message you here as soon as it's back.

Template 3: early access list

Hi {{name}}, you're in! You'll hear about new drops from {{store}} here before they go public.

For people who message you first

Template 4: first message

Hi {{name}}, thanks for messaging {{store}}! Ask us anything about products, orders or delivery. For order status, reply with your order number.

Template 5: away hours

Hi {{name}}, thanks for your message. We're available {{hours}} and will reply as soon as we're back. You can track orders anytime here: {{tracking_page}}

Template 6: from Instagram

Hi {{name}}, great to see you here from Instagram! Which product were you looking at? Send a screenshot and we'll help.

For returning customers

Template 7: welcome back

Welcome back, {{name}}! What can we help you with today? Reply with your order number for anything about a past order.

Template 8: after first order

Hi {{name}}, thanks for your first order with {{store}}. Save this chat: it's where you'll get updates and can reach us any time.

Template 9: bonus, opt-out confirmed

Done, {{name}}. You won't receive offers from {{store}} on WhatsApp any more. You'll still get updates about orders you place.

Templates or automated replies?

Messages you send first, such as an opt-in confirmation or a restock alert, must be approved templates. Replies to a customer who messaged you, such as the first-message and away-hours replies, are sent inside the customer service window. Meta gives each number 1,000 free service messages a month, so automated replies cost little or nothing for most stores.

Write welcomes that set expectations

  • Say what they will receive and roughly how often.
  • Say how to opt out, in the first message.
  • Give one next step, not a menu of ten.
  • Sign with the store name buyers know.

Frequently asked questions

What should a WhatsApp welcome message say?

Who you are, what the customer will receive and how often, how to opt out, and one next step.

Do I need template approval for a welcome message?

Yes, if you send it first, such as after an opt-in. Replies to a customer who messaged you are sent within the service window.

How long should a welcome message be?

Two to four short lines. Say who you are, what they will get, and how to stop or get help.

Are automated WhatsApp replies free?

Replies within the customer service window are service messages. Meta gives each number 1,000 free a month and charges after that.
