The best Instagram automation for Shopify stores

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is our top pick for Shopify stores on Instagram: comments can trigger a carousel of 2 to 10 Shopify products in one DM, with unlimited DMs on every plan. LinkDM is a simple option for one product link plus a coupon, and InstantDM suits stores that want phone numbers for follow-up.

Shopify stores get traffic from Instagram in two ways: the link in bio, which most people never tap, and the DM, which they do. A comment-to-DM that sends the exact product page turns a casual comment into a product view.

This ranking is narrower than a general list. It asks one question: how well does each tool get a commenter from your reel to the right Shopify product page?

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Several Shopify products in one DM

Tadlo AI's comment-to-product carousel sends 2 to 10 product cards in one DM, each linking to its product page. A "shop the look" reel can answer one comment with every item in it.

Its link-in-bio page collects emails as well as linking out, and the analytics show bio clicks and DM clicks per flow, so you can see which reel sent traffic to your store. The free plan includes all of this for one account.

Price: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits).

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Verified follow gate: checks through Instagram's API whether the commenter really follows you before sending the link
  • Comment-to-DM on any comment or chosen keywords, story reply automation and DM keyword flows in a visual flow builder
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • Connects with Instagram Business Login on the official API, so no Facebook Page is needed
  • AI reel analysis and analytics on paid plans

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Newer than ManyChat, so the template library and tutorial community are smaller
  • The Free plan covers one Instagram account
  • The Free plan adds a "via Tadlo" line to DMs and limits lead export to 100 leads

From comment to checkout

  1. The reel shows the product and says the keyword: "Comment SHOP for the link".
  2. The DM arrives in seconds with the product card or carousel.
  3. The buyer taps through to the Shopify product page, with the size or variant ready to pick.
  4. A follow-up offers WhatsApp for questions about delivery or COD.
  5. Analytics show which reel and which product got the clicks.

Which posts to automate

Start with posts that already get product questions: new arrivals, restocks, and reels that show a product in use. Add a comment-to-DM to each with a clear keyword in the caption and the video. Leave brand storytelling posts without automation; the comments there are reactions, not purchase intent, and a sales DM can feel out of place.

Link to the right page

  • Link to the product page, not the home page; every extra tap loses buyers.
  • For a collection reel, use a carousel or a collection page.
  • Add UTM parameters to your links so Shopify analytics attribute sales to the reel.
  • Check links still work after you change a product handle in Shopify.

Frequently asked questions

How do I send Shopify product links from Instagram comments?

Use a comment-to-DM tool with a keyword rule that sends the product page link. Tadlo AI can send up to 10 products as a carousel in one DM.

Can Instagram automation connect to my Shopify store?

The DM links to your Shopify product pages. For order updates after purchase, use a WhatsApp tool connected to Shopify.

What is the best Instagram automation for Shopify?

Tadlo AI is our top pick for its product carousels and unlimited DMs on every plan.
