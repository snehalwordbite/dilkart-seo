WhatsApp automation for D2C brands: a complete playbook

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Set up WhatsApp in this order: number and opt-in, order updates, COD confirmation, cart recovery, then campaigns. For most Indian D2C brands we recommend Tadlo AI for the job, because its Growth plan covers all five with no markup on Meta's charges.

Most D2C brands start WhatsApp backwards: they send a sale broadcast first, get blocked by a few people, and wonder why their quality rating dropped. The brands that get the most from WhatsApp start with the messages buyers want, order updates, and earn the right to send promotions later.

This playbook takes you from zero to a working setup in five stages, in the order that protects your number and pays back fastest.

Stage 1: get on the WhatsApp Business Platform

  1. Choose a provider that creates the WhatsApp Business Account inside your own Meta business account, so the number stays yours.
  2. Pick the number: a new one, or one moved from the WhatsApp Business app (it then stops working in the app).
  3. Set your display name and profile exactly as customers know your brand.
  4. Verify your Meta business account if you can; it affects your messaging limits over time.
  5. Add a WhatsApp opt-in at checkout, worded with your brand name, and keep a record of it.

Stage 2: order updates first

Order confirmation, shipped, out for delivery and delivered are utility messages: ₹0.115 each to Indian numbers from 1 October 2026, and they are the messages buyers thank you for. Get these four templates approved, connect your Shopify or WooCommerce store, and switch them on before anything else. They cut "where is my order" questions and warm up your number with messages people read.

Stage 3: COD confirmation

If cash on delivery is a large share of your orders, add a confirmation right after each COD order: confirm, cancel, or pay now instead. It is a utility message too, and every cancelled-before-dispatch order is a return you did not pay for.

Stage 4: abandoned cart recovery

Cart reminders are marketing messages, ₹0.8631 each to Indian numbers. Send one reminder a few hours after the cart is left, stop the moment the order is placed, and at most one follow-up. Only message people who opted in at checkout.

Stage 5: your first campaign

  1. Choose a segment, not your whole list: past buyers of a category, or people who opted in recently.
  2. Write one clear offer with an end date and one link.
  3. Check the cost before you send. On a tool with no markup, it is Meta's rate times the recipients.
  4. Send in the morning or early evening, when replies can be answered.
  5. Watch opt-outs and your quality rating for the next two days before sending again.

Protect your number

Every template has a quality rating based on usage, customer feedback and engagement; low ratings can lead to template pacing or pausing. New business portfolios start with a messaging limit of 250 people in a moving 24-hour period, which can rise to 2,000, 10,000, 100,000 and then unlimited as you send high-quality messages and use at least half of your limit in 7 days.

In practice: start with utility messages, keep marketing to opted-in segments, make opting out easy, and never buy contact lists.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start WhatsApp automation for my D2C brand?

Get your number on the WhatsApp Business Platform through a provider, add opt-in at checkout, then switch on order updates before anything promotional.

Which WhatsApp messages should a D2C brand automate first?

Order confirmation and shipping updates, then COD confirmation, then abandoned cart reminders, then campaigns.

Can I keep using the WhatsApp Business app?

Not on the same number. A number moved to the WhatsApp Business Platform stops working in the app.
