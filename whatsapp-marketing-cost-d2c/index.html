What WhatsApp costs a D2C brand each month

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

A D2C brand's WhatsApp bill is Meta's message charges plus the tool's plan, plus any markup. From 1 October 2026, Meta charges ₹0.8631 per marketing and ₹0.115 per utility message to Indian numbers. For a month of 10,000 marketing and 20,000 utility messages that is about ₹10,931. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job, because it adds no markup to that figure.

WhatsApp is cheap per message and easy to overspend on in total. The bill has three parts that move separately, and most brands only watch one of them.

This guide shows how to estimate each part from your own order numbers.

Estimate your utility messages

  1. Count your monthly orders.
  2. Multiply by the number of updates per order: confirmation, shipped, out for delivery, delivered.
  3. Add a COD confirmation for each COD order.
  4. Multiply the total by ₹0.115.

Estimate your marketing messages

  1. Count abandoned carts you will remind.
  2. Add each campaign's recipients for the month.
  3. Multiply the total by ₹0.8631.

Where brands overspend

  • Order updates sent as marketing templates, at about seven times the utility price.
  • Campaigns to the whole list instead of segments.
  • Several cart follow-ups per cart.
  • A markup they never checked.

Where brands save

  • Replying inside the customer service window, where the first 1,000 service messages a month are free.
  • Segmenting campaigns to buyers likely to act.
  • Choosing a tool with no markup as volume grows.

Plan fees by volume

Tools price plans on different things: contacts reached, seats, or features. Tadlo AI's plans are priced on unique contacts engaged per month: ₹999 for 1,000 contacts on Starter, ₹2,499 for 10,000 on Growth, ₹5,999 for 40,000 on Pro. Pick the plan that covers your busiest month.

A quick estimate for a small store

Take a store with 1,000 orders a month, three utility updates per order and one cart reminder for each of 1,500 carts. That is 3,000 utility messages and 1,500 marketing messages, plus any campaigns. Multiply each by its rate, add your tool's plan, and you have a monthly budget you can check against the invoice.

Track it monthly

Each month, compare message counts by category with your orders. If marketing messages grow faster than orders, your campaigns are getting less efficient.

Frequently asked questions

How much does WhatsApp marketing cost in India?

₹0.8631 per marketing message and ₹0.115 per utility message to Indian numbers from 1 October 2026, plus your tool's plan and any markup.

Are WhatsApp order updates cheaper than marketing?

Yes, as utility messages they cost ₹0.115 against ₹0.8631 for marketing, about seven times less.

Are any WhatsApp messages free?

Meta gives each number 1,000 free service messages a month, which are replies to customers who messaged you first.

Does WhatsApp charge for messages that are not delivered?

No. Meta charges per delivered message, so messages that fail to deliver are not billed.

Is GST charged on WhatsApp tools?

Tool plans in India are usually quoted excluding GST. Tadlo AI's prices exclude GST, so add it when you budget.

How do I lower my WhatsApp bill?

Use utility templates for order messages, segment campaigns, reply within the service window, and choose a tool without a markup.
