How to connect WooCommerce to WhatsApp, step by step

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Connect WooCommerce to a WhatsApp Business Platform provider, make your checkout collect phone numbers with country codes, map order statuses to approved templates, and switch on updates before campaigns. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job; WooCommerce order events, COD confirmation and cart recovery are included on its Growth plan.

WooCommerce stores have more moving parts than Shopify: plugins, custom order statuses, caching and hosting all affect whether events reach your WhatsApp tool. Most setup problems come from those, not from WhatsApp.

This guide covers the WooCommerce-specific steps, then the automations to switch on.

Prepare your checkout

  • Make the phone field required and validate the country code.
  • Add a WhatsApp opt-in checkbox that names your store.
  • Check that the phone field saves to the order, not only to the customer profile.

Connect the store

  1. Start Meta's signup inside your WhatsApp provider and connect your number.
  2. Install the provider's WooCommerce plugin or connect with API keys, following its instructions.
  3. Grant read access to orders and, for cart recovery, to carts or checkouts.
  4. Place a test order and confirm the event arrives in the provider.

Custom statuses and shipping plugins

Many WooCommerce stores add a shipped status through a shipping plugin. Make sure your WhatsApp tool can see it; otherwise the shipped update will never fire. If it cannot, use the completed status for a combined shipped message and skip the delivered one.

Caching and security plugins

Exclude cart and checkout pages from page caching, and allow your WhatsApp provider's requests through any security plugin. Blocked requests are the most common reason cart recovery silently stops working on WooCommerce.

Templates to approve first

  • Order confirmation (utility).
  • COD confirmation with buttons (utility).
  • Shipped with tracking (utility).
  • Cart reminder (marketing).

Switch on in this order

  1. Order confirmations.
  2. COD confirmation, if you offer cash on delivery.
  3. Shipped and delivered updates.
  4. Cart recovery, with a stop rule tied to new orders.
  5. Campaigns, only to opted-in customers.

Turn off old notifications

If you used a WhatsApp notification plugin before, switch it off once the new setup is live, or buyers will receive two messages for every order.

Testing checklist

  • A prepaid order and a COD order each trigger the right message.
  • The shipped message fires when your shipping plugin marks the order.
  • A cart left on a phone receives one reminder, and none after ordering.
  • Variables such as names and totals display correctly.

Costs

Order messages are utility, ₹0.115 each to Indian numbers from 1 October 2026; cart reminders and campaigns are marketing at ₹0.8631. Check whether your provider adds a markup to either.

Troubleshooting

  • No messages at all: template not approved, or the number lacks a country code.
  • Cart reminders not firing: cart events blocked by caching or security plugins.
  • Shipped message missing: the shipped status is not visible to the provider.
  • Duplicates: an old plugin still sending.

Frequently asked questions

How do I connect WhatsApp to WooCommerce?

Use a WhatsApp Business Platform provider with a WooCommerce plugin or integration, map order statuses to approved templates, and add opt-in at checkout.

Do I need a developer to connect WooCommerce to WhatsApp?

Usually not. Most providers offer a plugin or step-by-step connection. A developer helps if your store uses custom order statuses or heavy caching.

Why are my WooCommerce WhatsApp messages not sending?

Usually a template that is not approved, phone numbers without a country code, or caching and security plugins blocking events.

Can WooCommerce send COD confirmations on WhatsApp?

Yes, through a provider that reads COD orders. Tadlo AI asks buyers to confirm, cancel or switch to prepaid from its Growth plan.

Which WhatsApp tool works best with WooCommerce?

We recommend Tadlo AI, which includes WooCommerce events from Growth. WATI has an official WooCommerce integration, and AiSensy includes it on paid plans.
