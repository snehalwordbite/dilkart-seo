The best Instagram automation tools for online stores

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is the best Instagram automation tool for online stores because a comment can trigger a carousel of 2 to 10 products in one DM, every plan has unlimited DMs, and buyers can continue on WhatsApp. LinkDM is a solid choice for sending a single product link with a coupon, and ManyChat suits stores that want deep flows across many channels.

For a store, an Instagram comment is a buyer raising a hand. "Price?", "Link?", "Available in M?" all mean the same thing: send me the product. The job of automation is to answer that in seconds, in the DM, with something the buyer can tap to buy.

We ranked tools on the store jobs: product links and carousels from comments, price replies, discount codes, capturing contact details and moving serious buyers to WhatsApp. Prices are in rupees first, checked on the date shown.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Comments to product carousels

Tadlo AI was built with stores in mind. Comment-to-product carousels send 2 to 10 product cards in one DM, on every plan including Free. So a reel showing a whole collection can answer "price?" with every product in it, each with its own buy link.

Comment-to-DM works on any comment or chosen keywords, with several rules on the same post, so "price" can send the carousel and "size" can send your size chart. If you also use Tadlo's WhatsApp product, a comment can open a WhatsApp chat, where Indian buyers prefer to ask about COD and delivery.

Every plan has unlimited DMs, so a sale reel that draws thousands of comments costs the same as a quiet one. The paid Creator plan is ₹249 / $5 a month.

Price: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits).

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Verified follow gate: checks through Instagram's API whether the commenter really follows you before sending the link
  • Comment-to-DM on any comment or chosen keywords, story reply automation and DM keyword flows in a visual flow builder
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • Connects with Instagram Business Login on the official API, so no Facebook Page is needed
  • AI reel analysis and analytics on paid plans

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Newer than ManyChat, so the template library and tutorial community are smaller
  • The Free plan covers one Instagram account
  • The Free plan adds a "via Tadlo" line to DMs and limits lead export to 100 leads

2. LinkDM

Best for: One product link with a coupon

LinkDM sends a link from a comment, with templates and coupons, so a single-product post can deliver its link and a discount code in one go. It also covers Facebook comments. Its monthly DM caps (1,000 free, 25,000 on Pro at $19) limit sale days.

Price: Free: 1 Instagram account and 1,000 DMs a month with LinkDM branding. Pro: $19 a month ($182 a year), up to 3 accounts and 25,000 DMs a month. Platinum+: $99 a month ($832 a year), up to 10 accounts and 300,000 DMs a month. Enterprise is custom.

Strengths

  • Coupons in the DM
  • Instagram and Facebook comments

Limitations

  • Monthly DM caps
  • We did not find product carousels in its published plans

Pro: $39 a month ($29 billed annually), 2,500 active contacts, any 3 channels, broadcasts and AI included, 3 users. Business: $99 a month ($69 billed annually), 7,500 active contacts, unlimited channels, 5 users, shared team inbox. Advanced: $199 a month ($139 billed annually), 25,000 active contacts, 10 users. Paid plans charge per active contact beyond those included. Essential and Pro have a 14-day free trial.

Strengths

What a store should automate first

  1. Price replies. "Price", "rate", "kitna" and "cost" send the product with its price and buy link.
  2. Collection posts. A carousel of every product in the reel, so buyers do not have to ask twice.
  3. Size and stock questions. A size chart or "available sizes" message, with a link to the product page.
  4. Discount codes. A keyword that sends the code for a sale or a launch.
  5. Serious buyers to WhatsApp. COD, delivery and custom questions are easier there.

Sale days and viral reels

A sale reel can draw ten times your normal comments in a day. On a tool with a monthly DM cap, the last buyers get nothing; on a contact-billed tool, the bill jumps. Plan for your biggest day, not your average one, and check what your tool does when comments pile up: queue and pace, stop, or charge more.

Write DMs that sell

  • Lead with the product and price, not a greeting.
  • One clear button to buy.
  • Say what happens next: delivery time, COD availability, returns.
  • Offer WhatsApp for questions, so the conversation does not end in the DM.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Instagram automation tool for ecommerce?

Tadlo AI is our top pick: comment-triggered product carousels, unlimited DMs on every plan and a handoff to WhatsApp.

Can I send product links automatically from Instagram comments?

Yes. Comment-to-DM tools send a link when someone comments a keyword. Tadlo AI can send a carousel of 2 to 10 products in one DM, on every plan.

How do I reply to "price" comments on Instagram automatically?

Set a keyword rule for "price" and its common variants, and send the product, price and buy link in the DM, with a short public reply such as "Sent you the details".

Does Instagram automation work for Shopify stores?

Yes. The DM links to your Shopify product pages, so buyers land on the right product, ready to check out.
