How to move Instagram commenters to WhatsApp

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Add a step to your Instagram comment-to-DM that opens a WhatsApp chat with your store, with the product already in the message. Buyers who want to ask about COD, size or delivery then continue where they prefer to talk. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job; its Instagram comments can open WhatsApp chats when you have paid plans on both its products.

Indian buyers discover on Instagram and decide on WhatsApp. They see the reel, comment "price", and then want to ask three more questions before paying. Instagram DMs work for the first answer; WhatsApp works better for the conversation that follows, and it is where order updates will arrive later anyway.

This guide shows how to make that move in one tap, and what to automate on each side.

Why move the conversation

  • Buyers already use WhatsApp for shopping questions and keep those chats.
  • No 24-hour wall. Instagram limits messaging to 24 hours after the buyer's last message; on WhatsApp, an opted-in buyer can receive order updates and later campaigns.
  • One thread for the whole order: question, confirmation, shipping, delivery.
  • Your team works one inbox instead of two.

The flow

  1. The buyer comments a keyword on your reel.
  2. The Instagram DM sends the product, price and a button: "Ask on WhatsApp".
  3. The button opens a WhatsApp chat with your store, with the product named in the first message.
  4. Your WhatsApp side answers instantly or routes to a person, and captures the buyer's opt-in.
  5. Once they order, confirmations and updates arrive in the same chat.

Do not lose people in the jump

  • Give the price in the Instagram DM first; buyers should not have to switch apps just to learn it.
  • Pre-fill the WhatsApp message with the product name, so the buyer does not have to type.
  • Reply on WhatsApp within minutes during business hours; a slow first reply undoes the move.
  • Keep the Instagram link for people who would rather buy straight away.

Opt-in on WhatsApp

A buyer who messages you first can get replies in the customer service window. To send them order updates and campaigns later, collect their opt-in clearly, naming your store. Meta requires businesses to collect opt-in that clearly states the person is agreeing to receive messages from the business, and names the business.

Costs

Replies inside the customer service window are service messages; Meta gives each number 1,000 free a month and charges from the 1,001st. Order updates later are utility messages, ₹0.115 each to Indian numbers. Instagram DMs carry no Meta per-message charge.

Staffing the WhatsApp side

Moving buyers to WhatsApp only works if someone answers there. Set business hours, an away message for outside them, and quick replies for the top questions. Automations can answer COD and delivery basics; a person should take sizing, custom requests and anything about a problem order.

Measuring the handoff

Track how many Instagram DMs led to a WhatsApp chat, how many chats led to an order, and how many orders came from buyers who started on Instagram. Tadlo AI's revenue attribution on WhatsApp Pro reports orders from conversations.
