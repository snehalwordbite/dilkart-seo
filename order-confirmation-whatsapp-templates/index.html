WhatsApp order confirmation templates

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Below are 8 WhatsApp order confirmation templates, written as utility messages with variables like {{name}}, {{order_id}} and {{total}}. Keep them free of offers so they stay at the utility rate. We recommend Tadlo AI for sending them; its Growth plan sends order confirmations from Shopify and WooCommerce with no markup.

An order confirmation is the message buyers are waiting for, and the cheapest one you send: utility messages to Indian numbers cost ₹0.115 each from 1 October 2026. Each template below covers a different kind of order. Pick the ones that match how you sell, replace the variables with your tool's field names, and submit them as utility templates.

Standard orders

Template 1: prepaid order

Hi {{name}}, thank you for your order {{order_id}} at {{store}}!
Items: {{items}}
Paid: {{total}}
We'll message you here when it ships, usually within {{dispatch_days}} days.

Template 2: cash on delivery

Hi {{name}}, your order {{order_id}} at {{store}} is placed.
Items: {{items}}
Pay on delivery: {{total}}
We'll confirm dispatch here. Reply if anything needs changing.

Template 3: short version

Order {{order_id}} confirmed, {{name}}. Total {{total}}. We'll update you here when it ships.

Template 4: with support contact

Hi {{name}}, we've received order {{order_id}} for {{total}}. For any question about this order, reply to this chat and our team will help.

Special orders

Template 5: pre-order

Hi {{name}}, your pre-order {{order_id}} for {{product_name}} is confirmed. It is expected to ship by {{ship_date}}. We'll message you here when it does.

Template 6: made to order

Hi {{name}}, we've started work on your order {{order_id}} ({{product_name}}). It usually takes {{making_days}} days to make, and we'll message you when it's ready to ship.

Template 7: split shipment

Hi {{name}}, your order {{order_id}} will arrive in {{parcel_count}} parcels. We'll send tracking for each one here as it ships.

Template 8: gift order

Hi {{name}}, your gift order {{order_id}} is confirmed and will be delivered to {{recipient_name}} in {{city}}. Prices will not be shown on the packing slip.

How to use these templates

  1. Choose the templates that match your order types; most stores need 1 or 2, plus 5 to 8 if they apply.
  2. Replace the variables with your tool's field names.
  3. Submit them in the utility category.
  4. Map each to the right order event in your store.
  5. Test with a real order and check every variable fills.

Keep them in the utility category

  • No discount codes or offers for the next order.
  • No links to other products.
  • Only information about the order the buyer placed.
  • A clear way to get help with this order.

Small touches that build trust

  • Use the buyer's first name, not their full name.
  • Repeat the amount for COD orders so there are no surprises at the door.
  • Give a dispatch window you can meet.
  • Sign with the store name buyers know.

Frequently asked questions

What should a WhatsApp order confirmation include?

Order number, items, total, payment method and when it will ship, plus a way to get help.

Is an order confirmation a utility template?

Yes, as long as it contains no promotion. It costs ₹0.115 to an Indian number from 1 October 2026.

Can I use one template for every order?

Many stores do, with separate versions for cash on delivery and special cases like pre-orders.

Can I add my logo or a product image?

Templates can include a header image, such as your logo or the product photo. Keep the text factual so the template stays utility.
