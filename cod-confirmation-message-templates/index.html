COD confirmation message templates for WhatsApp

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Below are 8 COD confirmation templates for WhatsApp, with variables like {{name}}, {{order_id}} and {{total}}. Send the confirmation within minutes of each COD order, with buttons to confirm, cancel or pay now. We recommend Tadlo AI for sending them; its Growth plan runs this flow from Shopify and WooCommerce orders.

COD confirmations are utility templates as long as they stay transactional, so keep offers out of the main message. Add buttons when you submit: Confirm, Cancel and Pay now.

Templates 1 to 4 are confirmations; 5 to 8 handle reminders and replies.

Confirmations

Template 1: standard confirmation

Hi {{name}}, thanks for your order {{order_id}} from {{store}}.
Items: {{items}}
To pay on delivery: {{total}}
Please confirm your order using the buttons below.

Template 2: with address check

Hi {{name}}, please confirm your cash on delivery order {{order_id}} for {{total}}.
Delivery address: {{address}}
If anything is wrong, reply with the correct address.

Template 3: with pay-now option

Hi {{name}}, your order {{order_id}} ({{total}}) is ready to pack. Confirm to pay on delivery, or tap Pay now to pay online and skip handing over cash.

Template 4: high-value order

Hi {{name}}, before we dispatch your order {{order_id}} worth {{total}}, please confirm it below. Our team will call only if we cannot reach you here.

Reminders and replies

Template 5: reminder

Hi {{name}}, we're holding your order {{order_id}} until you confirm. Tap Confirm to send it today, or Cancel if you no longer need it.

Template 6: confirmed reply

Thanks {{name}}! Order {{order_id}} is confirmed and will be dispatched by {{dispatch_date}}. Please keep {{total}} ready on delivery.

Template 7: cancelled reply

Your order {{order_id}} has been cancelled, {{name}}. Nothing will be shipped. If this was a mistake, reply here and we'll help.

Template 8: paid online reply

Payment received for order {{order_id}}, {{name}}. Your order is now prepaid and will be dispatched by {{dispatch_date}}.

How to use these templates

  1. Pick one confirmation template for every COD order.
  2. Submit it as a utility template with Confirm, Cancel and Pay now buttons.
  3. Send it within minutes of the order.
  4. Use template 5 once if there is no reply, then follow your policy.
  5. Use 6, 7 or 8 to close the loop on each answer.

Adapting them to your store

Match the tone to your brand, but keep the structure: what the order is, what it costs, where it is going, and what to do now. If you ship in regional languages, submit a version of the confirmation in each language you use, since a buyer who cannot read the message cannot confirm it.

Keep them utility

  • No offers, coupons or product suggestions in these messages.
  • Facts only: order, amount, address, next step.
  • Clear buttons, so replies are one tap.

Frequently asked questions

What should a COD confirmation message include?

The order number, items, amount to pay, delivery address and buttons to confirm, cancel or pay now.

Is a COD confirmation a utility or marketing template?

Utility, as long as it contains no promotion. It costs ₹0.115 to an Indian number from 1 October 2026.

What if the customer does not reply?

Send one reminder, then follow your own rule for unconfirmed orders.

Can I send COD confirmations in Hindi?

Yes. Submit a separate template in each language you use, and choose the language for each buyer based on how they shop with you.
