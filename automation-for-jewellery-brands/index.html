WhatsApp and Instagram automation for jewellery brands

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is our top pick for jewellery brands because it answers Instagram price enquiries with product carousels, confirms high-value COD orders on WhatsApp, and sends payment links in chat on its Pro plan. Gallabox suits jewellery brands whose sales team closes deals on WhatsApp, and Interakt suits Shopify jewellery stores.

Jewellery is a trust sale. Buyers want to see the piece move in light, ask about purity and certification, check return terms and often negotiate before paying. Orders are high-value, so a refused COD parcel costs more than in most categories.

Automation in jewellery is less about volume and more about speed and reassurance: answer the first question instantly, then get a person into the conversation for the rest.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Jewellery brands that sell on Instagram and confirm on WhatsApp

On Instagram, Tadlo AI answers "price?" with a carousel of the pieces in the reel, and can ask for an email inside the DM for buyers who want the catalogue. A comment can open a WhatsApp chat when you have paid plans on both products, which is where jewellery buyers ask for videos and certificates.

On WhatsApp, the Growth plan confirms COD orders before dispatch, important when a single refused parcel is expensive, and Pro at ₹5,999 adds payment links in chat, so a buyer who is ready can pay in the conversation instead of at the door.

Price: Instagram: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits). WhatsApp: Free forever plan with up to 10 messages a day. Starter ₹999, Growth ₹2,499, Pro ₹5,999 and Scale ₹11,999 a month, priced on unique contacts engaged per month. Meta's message charges are billed by Meta directly to your own account, and Tadlo adds no markup. Prices exclude GST.

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • No markup on Meta's message charges: Meta bills your own WhatsApp Business Account directly, and Tadlo only charges a flat plan
  • Built for D2C: abandoned cart recovery, COD to prepaid nudges and order updates from Shopify and WooCommerce (Growth plan and above)
  • Instagram comments to WhatsApp chats, which most WhatsApp-only tools cannot do

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Newer than AiSensy, WATI or Interakt, with a shorter track record
  • Commerce automations start on the Growth plan (₹2,499 a month)
  • Tadlo AI is onboarding brands in stages, and you request access on its WhatsApp page.

2. Gallabox

Best for: Sales teams closing high-ticket orders

Gallabox gives a sales team CRM-style tools inside WhatsApp, a good fit for jewellery brands where a person handles every serious enquiry. Plans start at ₹2,999 a month billed quarterly.

Price: Basic ₹2,999, Essential ₹6,999 and Advanced ₹16,999 a month, billed quarterly, with a 7-day free trial and no card needed. Meta's message charges appear on your Gallabox invoice with a markup; Gallabox does not publish the percentage.

Strengths

  • CRM features for sales reps
  • Shopify listed

Limitations

  • Markup percentage not published
  • Billed quarterly

3. Interakt

Best for: Shopify jewellery stores

Interakt combines Shopify on every WhatsApp plan with Razorpay or PayU as free app connections, useful for sending payment links.

Price: WhatsApp plans: Growth ₹2,799 and Advanced ₹3,799 a month plus taxes; Enterprise on request with no markup. The ₹999 Starter plan covers Instagram only. Interakt charges its own per-message rates, which sit above Meta's: marketing ₹0.970 / ₹0.958 / ₹0.949 and utility ₹0.160 / ₹0.150 / ₹0.140 on Starter, Growth and Advanced. 14-day free trial.

Strengths

  • Payment app connections
  • Shopify on every WhatsApp plan

Limitations

  • Markup below Enterprise

4. WATI

Best for: Brands with a dedicated support desk

WATI's mature inbox suits jewellery brands with a team answering detailed questions about purity, sizing and returns.

Price: Growth ₹2,999, Pro ₹6,499 and Business ₹18,499 a month billed monthly, with savings of up to 10% on quarterly and up to about 25% on annual billing. Growth includes 3 users with no additional users; Pro and Business include 5, with extra users at ₹1,299 and ₹2,199 a month each. Template and service messages are charged on top, with a markup on Meta's rates. A free trial is available.

Strengths

Limitations

5. ManyChat

Best for: Guided product finders

Pro: $39 a month ($29 billed annually), 2,500 active contacts, any 3 channels, broadcasts and AI included, 3 users. Business: $99 a month ($69 billed annually), 7,500 active contacts, unlimited channels, 5 users, shared team inbox. Advanced: $199 a month ($139 billed annually), 25,000 active contacts, 10 users. Paid plans charge per active contact beyond those included. Essential and Pro have a 14-day free trial.

Trust before payment

  • Answer purity, certification and return questions in the first reply, briefly and accurately.
  • Offer a live video on WhatsApp for serious buyers; it does more than any photo.
  • Show the full price including any making charges, so there are no surprises at checkout.
  • Have a named person take over serious conversations; jewellery buyers want to know who they are dealing with.

High-value COD

A refused jewellery parcel ties up expensive stock and costs insured shipping both ways. Confirm every COD order on WhatsApp before dispatch, show the order value and address, and offer a payment link for buyers who prefer to pay now. Hold unconfirmed high-value orders until you hear back, according to your policy.

Festive and wedding seasons

Demand peaks around festivals and weddings. Prepare carousels for gifting and bridal collections, and broadcast only to buyers who opted in, with a clear offer and an easy way to ask a question.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best automation tool for a jewellery brand?

Tadlo AI is our top pick: Instagram product carousels, WhatsApp COD confirmation, and payment links in chat on its Pro plan.

How do jewellery brands handle video enquiries on WhatsApp?

Route video requests to a person who sends a short live video from the store or studio. Automation should hand off quickly, not try to replace this.

Should jewellery brands offer COD?

Many do, because buyers expect it. Confirming every COD order before dispatch and offering a payment link reduces the cost of refused parcels.
