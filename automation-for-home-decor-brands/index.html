Instagram and WhatsApp automation for home decor brands

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is our top pick for home decor brands because a comment on a room reel can return a carousel of every item in it, and WhatsApp confirms bulky COD orders before they ship. Gallabox suits furniture brands whose sales team follows up enquiries, and Interakt suits Shopify decor stores.

Home decor sells through rooms, not products. A reel of a styled living room brings comments asking about the lamp, the rug and the cushion covers at once, plus questions about dimensions, material and delivery for large items.

Decor brands also carry two extra risks: bulky orders are expensive to ship back, and buyers often need to check measurements before committing. Good automation answers the "what is that?" question instantly and moves measurement questions to a person.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Room reels that sell several products

Tadlo AI's comment-to-product carousel sends 2 to 10 product cards in one DM, so "LINK" on a room reel returns the lamp, rug and cushions together, each with its own page. A second keyword such as "SIZE" can send a dimensions sheet.

On WhatsApp, the Growth plan confirms COD orders before dispatch, important for bulky items that cost a lot to return, and sends shipping updates through to delivery. Comments can open WhatsApp chats when you have paid plans on both products, for buyers who want to send a photo of their room.

Price: Instagram: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits). WhatsApp: Free forever plan with up to 10 messages a day. Starter ₹999, Growth ₹2,499, Pro ₹5,999 and Scale ₹11,999 a month, priced on unique contacts engaged per month. Meta's message charges are billed by Meta directly to your own account, and Tadlo adds no markup. Prices exclude GST.

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • No markup on Meta's message charges: Meta bills your own WhatsApp Business Account directly, and Tadlo only charges a flat plan
  • Built for D2C: abandoned cart recovery, COD to prepaid nudges and order updates from Shopify and WooCommerce (Growth plan and above)
  • Instagram comments to WhatsApp chats, which most WhatsApp-only tools cannot do

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Newer than AiSensy, WATI or Interakt, with a shorter track record
  • Commerce automations start on the Growth plan (₹2,499 a month)
  • Tadlo AI is onboarding brands in stages, and you request access on its WhatsApp page.

2. Gallabox

Best for: Furniture brands with a sales team

Gallabox's CRM-style tools suit furniture brands where a rep follows up every serious enquiry with photos and quotes.

Price: Basic ₹2,999, Essential ₹6,999 and Advanced ₹16,999 a month, billed quarterly, with a 7-day free trial and no card needed. Meta's message charges appear on your Gallabox invoice with a markup; Gallabox does not publish the percentage.

Strengths

  • CRM features
  • 7-day trial, no card

Limitations

  • Markup percentage not published
  • Billed quarterly

3. Interakt

Best for: Shopify decor stores

Interakt includes Shopify on every WhatsApp plan, with payment app connections for prepaid links.

Price: WhatsApp plans: Growth ₹2,799 and Advanced ₹3,799 a month plus taxes; Enterprise on request with no markup. The ₹999 Starter plan covers Instagram only. Interakt charges its own per-message rates, which sit above Meta's: marketing ₹0.970 / ₹0.958 / ₹0.949 and utility ₹0.160 / ₹0.150 / ₹0.140 on Starter, Growth and Advanced. 14-day free trial.

Strengths

  • Shopify on every WhatsApp plan
  • Payment app connections

Limitations

  • Markup below Enterprise

4. LinkDM

Best for: Single-product posts

LinkDM suits decor brands that post one product at a time and want a link and coupon in the DM.

Price: Free: 1 Instagram account and 1,000 DMs a month with LinkDM branding. Pro: $19 a month ($182 a year), up to 3 accounts and 25,000 DMs a month. Platinum+: $99 a month ($832 a year), up to 10 accounts and 300,000 DMs a month. Enterprise is custom.

Strengths

  • Coupons
  • Simple setup

Limitations

  • Monthly DM caps
  • We did not find product carousels in its published plans

5. AiSensy

Best for: Seasonal sale campaigns

AiSensy suits decor brands that run frequent sale broadcasts, with Shopify and WooCommerce on paid plans.

Price: A forever-free plan gives WhatsApp API access with ₹1000 in ad credits and ₹50 in conversation credits, for 1 agent, without broadcasts or integrations. Paid plans were Basic ₹1,500 and Pro ₹3,200 a month according to AiSensy's blog on 17 February 2025. Calculated from AiSensy's published India rates (marketing ₹1.09, utility ₹0.145) against Meta's rates, its message charges are about 26% higher than Meta's.

Strengths

  • Strong broadcasts

Limitations

  • About 26% above Meta's rates
  • Wallet top-ups

Dimensions save returns

Most decor returns come down to size: the rug was smaller, the shelf deeper, the colour different in daylight. Send dimensions in the first reply, with a simple guide to measuring the space, and invite buyers to send a photo on WhatsApp if unsure. A minute of a person's time on WhatsApp costs far less than a returned sofa.

Bulky orders and COD

Large items cost more to ship and more to return. Confirm every COD order for bulky items before dispatch, state the delivery window, and ask the buyer to confirm someone will be home. Offer a payment link for buyers ready to pay now.

Catalogues and lead capture

Decor buyers often browse for weeks before buying. Offer your catalogue by keyword and ask for an email in the DM, so you can follow up when a new collection lands. Tadlo AI keeps those emails in a leads inbox with the source post.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best automation tool for a home decor brand?

Tadlo AI is our top pick: comment-triggered carousels for room reels and WhatsApp COD confirmation for bulky orders.

How do I send links for every product in a room reel?

Use a comment-to-product carousel. Tadlo AI sends 2 to 10 product cards in one DM, on every plan.

How can decor brands reduce returns?

Send dimensions and materials in the first reply, invite room photos on WhatsApp, and confirm bulky COD orders before dispatch.
