WhatsApp and Instagram automation for kids and baby brands

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is our top pick for kids and baby brands: it answers parents' size and product questions on Instagram and handles COD, order updates and broadcasts on WhatsApp with no markup. Reorder messages for diapers and essentials are sent as opted-in broadcasts or from your subscription system; we did not find scheduled reorder reminders in Tadlo's published plans.

Parents buy carefully and often: sizes change as children grow, essentials run out fast, and safety questions matter more than in any other category. They also tend to ask on WhatsApp before buying.

This playbook ranks tools on the jobs kids and baby brands run, and is clear about what is automated.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Parents' questions and repeat essentials

On Instagram, a SIZE keyword can send a size-by-age chart and a product carousel, with unlimited DMs. On WhatsApp, the Growth plan confirms COD orders, sends order and delivery updates, recovers carts and runs broadcasts with the cost shown first.

Essentials like diapers and wipes are reordered often; send reorder messages as a monthly opted-in broadcast built from your order data. Reminders and follow-up sequences run from your own booking, subscription or CRM system; we did not find scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans.

Price: Instagram: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits). WhatsApp: Free forever plan with up to 10 messages a day. Starter ₹999, Growth ₹2,499, Pro ₹5,999 and Scale ₹11,999 a month, priced on unique contacts engaged per month. Meta's message charges are billed by Meta directly to your own account, and Tadlo adds no markup. Prices exclude GST.

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • No markup on Meta's message charges: Meta bills your own WhatsApp Business Account directly, and Tadlo only charges a flat plan
  • Built for D2C: abandoned cart recovery, COD to prepaid nudges and order updates from Shopify and WooCommerce (Growth plan and above)
  • Instagram comments to WhatsApp chats, which most WhatsApp-only tools cannot do

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Newer than AiSensy, WATI or Interakt, with a shorter track record
  • Commerce automations start on the Growth plan (₹2,499 a month)
  • Tadlo AI is onboarding brands in stages, and you request access on its WhatsApp page.

Visit Tadlo AI

2. Interakt

Best for: Established Shopify baby brands

For baby brands that already run on Shopify, Interakt brings ready-made store flows and payment apps such as Razorpay, which parents use for prepaid orders.

Price: WhatsApp plans: Growth ₹2,799 and Advanced ₹3,799 a month plus taxes; Enterprise on request with no markup. The ₹999 Starter plan covers Instagram only. Interakt charges its own per-message rates, which sit above Meta's: marketing ₹0.970 / ₹0.958 / ₹0.949 and utility ₹0.160 / ₹0.150 / ₹0.140 on Starter, Growth and Advanced. 14-day free trial.

Strengths

  • Familiar Shopify flows
  • Razorpay and PayU among free apps

Limitations

  • Own per-message rates above Meta's below Enterprise

Visit Interakt

3. WATI

Best for: Support-heavy brands

Parents ask a lot before buying a car seat or a pram. WATI's polished team inbox helps a support team answer those detailed questions quickly.

Price: Growth ₹2,999, Pro ₹6,499 and Business ₹18,499 a month billed monthly, with savings of up to 10% on quarterly and up to about 25% on annual billing. Growth includes 3 users with no additional users; Pro and Business include 5, with extra users at ₹1,299 and ₹2,199 a month each. Template and service messages are charged on top, with a markup on Meta's rates. A free trial is available.

Strengths

  • Polished inbox for pre-purchase questions

Limitations

  • Does not publish its markup percentage

Visit WATI

4. AiSensy

Best for: Sale-led brands

AiSensy suits brands that rely on sale campaigns, such as back-to-school.

Price: A forever-free plan gives WhatsApp API access with ₹1000 in ad credits and ₹50 in conversation credits, for 1 agent, without broadcasts or integrations. Paid plans were Basic ₹1,500 and Pro ₹3,200 a month according to AiSensy's blog on 17 February 2025. Calculated from AiSensy's published India rates (marketing ₹1.09, utility ₹0.145) against Meta's rates, its message charges are about 26% higher than Meta's.

Strengths

  • Strong broadcasts

Limitations

  • About 26% above Meta's rates

Visit AiSensy

5. LinkDM

Best for: Instagram product links

LinkDM sends product links and coupons from comments.

Price: Free: 1 Instagram account and 1,000 DMs a month with LinkDM branding. Pro: $19 a month ($182 a year), up to 3 accounts and 25,000 DMs a month. Platinum+: $99 a month ($832 a year), up to 10 accounts and 300,000 DMs a month. Enterprise is custom.

Strengths

  • Coupons

Limitations

  • Monthly DM caps
  • No WhatsApp: it says it only works with Instagram and Facebook

Gifting and baby showers

A large share of baby products are bought as gifts by relatives and friends, not parents. A GIFT keyword on Instagram can send a carousel of gift sets and ask the buyer for the baby's age, and on WhatsApp you can offer gift wrapping and a message card at confirmation. Gift buyers rarely know sizes, so suggest one size up.

Growing out of things

Children outgrow clothes and shoes every few months, which is a natural reason to hear from you again. A seasonal broadcast to opted-in parents, such as "winter sizes are in", with sizes one step up from their last order, is helpful rather than pushy.

Size by age, done well

Children's sizes vary by brand. Publish a size chart by age and by measurement, and say how each product fits. Fewer wrong sizes means fewer exchanges and happier parents.

Safety questions

  • Answer with the certifications and materials you actually have.
  • Never claim a product is completely safe in all situations.
  • Route medical questions, such as allergies or rashes, to a doctor.

Reorders, honestly

Diapers, wipes and formula are used up quickly. Each month, pull opted-in customers whose last essentials order was a few weeks ago and send one reorder broadcast with a direct link, adjusted for the size they last bought.

Which tool for which brand

  • Instagram-led, COD-heavy: Tadlo AI.
  • Shopify-first, established: Interakt.
  • Large support team: WATI.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best automation tool for a baby products brand?

Tadlo AI is our top pick: size answers on Instagram, and COD, order updates and broadcasts on WhatsApp with no markup.

Can I send diaper reorder reminders automatically?

We did not find scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans. Send reorder messages as opted-in broadcasts or use a subscription platform.

How should I answer parents' safety questions?

With the certifications and materials you hold, answered by a person; route medical questions to a doctor.

How do I reduce wrong-size orders?

Send a size chart by age and measurement, with fit notes, before purchase.
