How to send a product link when someone comments on Instagram

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Create a comment-to-DM rule on the post: pick a keyword, write a DM with the product and price, and attach the product link or a carousel. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job, because its comment-to-product carousel sends 2 to 10 product cards in one DM on every plan, with unlimited DMs.

"Link?" is the most common comment on a product reel, and Instagram does not let you put clickable links in captions. Comment-to-DM solves that: the link arrives privately, seconds after the comment.

This guide walks through the setup, then the choices that decide how many of those links get clicked.

Before you start

  • A professional Instagram account (creator or business).
  • Message access for connected tools turned on in Instagram's settings.
  • A comment-to-DM tool connected to your account.
  • The product page URLs you want to send, ideally with tracking parameters.

Set it up in five steps

  1. Pick the post or reel.
  2. Choose the trigger: a keyword such as LINK, SHOP or PRICE, plus the variants your buyers type.
  3. Write the DM: product name, price, one line on delivery.
  4. Attach the product link, or a carousel for posts with several products.
  5. Add a short public reply and switch it on; test from a second account.

Keywords that work

Short, obvious and spoken in the video: LINK, SHOP, PRICE. Add variants: "link pls", "price?", "prize", "rate", "kitna". If your audience comments in Hindi or Hinglish, include those spellings; a missed keyword is a missed buyer.

The DM that gets clicked

  • First line: the product and price.
  • One button, labelled with what happens: "View product" or "Buy now".
  • A line on delivery or COD, the next question every buyer has.
  • No long greeting before the link.

Several rules on one post

A single reel can carry more than one keyword. LINK can send the product, SIZE the size chart, and CODE a discount. Each rule sends its own DM, so the buyer gets exactly what they asked for. Keep it to three keywords per post at most, or people will not remember which to type.

Public replies

Reply publicly with a short, varied line such as "Sent!" or "Check your DMs". It shows other viewers the offer is real and nudges them to comment too. Rotate a few versions rather than posting the same one hundreds of times.

Keep old posts working

Product reels keep getting comments for weeks. Leave the rule on, and when a product sells out, change the DM to offer a restock alert or a similar product rather than a dead link.

Measure it

Watch DMs sent and link clicks per post, then orders using tracking parameters on your product links. Posts with many comments but few clicks usually need a better first line or a clearer button.

Frequently asked questions

How do I automatically send a link when someone comments on Instagram?

Use a comment-to-DM tool: set a keyword on the post and write a DM with the link. The link arrives in the commenter's DMs within seconds.

Can I send several products in one Instagram DM?

Yes, as a carousel. Tadlo AI sends 2 to 10 product cards in one DM, on every plan.

Can the DM link to a Shopify or WooCommerce product page?

Yes. Any product page URL works. Add tracking parameters so your store's analytics can attribute orders to the post.

Do I need a business account to send links from comments?

You need a professional Instagram account, either creator or business, with message access for connected tools turned on.

Why are my product links not being clicked?

Usually the first line of the DM does not show the product and price, or the button is unclear. Lead with the product and use one clear button.
