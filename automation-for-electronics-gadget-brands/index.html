WhatsApp and Instagram automation for electronics and gadget brands

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is our top pick for electronics and gadget brands because its WhatsApp plans send order and delivery updates from Shopify and WooCommerce, confirm COD orders, and run accessory campaigns with the cost shown before sending. WATI suits brands with a large support desk, and Interakt suits Shopify electronics stores.

Electronics buyers ask different questions from fashion buyers: specifications, compatibility, warranty and setup. After the sale, they want to know exactly when the parcel arrives, and many need help getting the device working.

That makes WhatsApp the more important channel for gadget brands, with Instagram handling discovery and specification questions on reels.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Gadget brands with heavy post-purchase messaging

On WhatsApp, Tadlo AI's Growth plan follows every order from cart to doorstep with utility messages, confirms COD orders, recovers abandoned carts and runs broadcasts with the cost shown before you send. The delivered update can carry your setup guide and warranty registration link.

On Instagram, "SPECS" or "PRICE" on a product reel can return the product card with key specifications and a link. Unlimited DMs on every plan cover launch-day spikes.

Price: Instagram: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits). WhatsApp: Free forever plan with up to 10 messages a day. Starter ₹999, Growth ₹2,499, Pro ₹5,999 and Scale ₹11,999 a month, priced on unique contacts engaged per month. Meta's message charges are billed by Meta directly to your own account, and Tadlo adds no markup. Prices exclude GST.

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • No markup on Meta's message charges: Meta bills your own WhatsApp Business Account directly, and Tadlo only charges a flat plan
  • Built for D2C: abandoned cart recovery, COD to prepaid nudges and order updates from Shopify and WooCommerce (Growth plan and above)
  • Instagram comments to WhatsApp chats, which most WhatsApp-only tools cannot do

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Newer than AiSensy, WATI or Interakt, with a shorter track record
  • Commerce automations start on the Growth plan (₹2,499 a month)
  • Tadlo AI is onboarding brands in stages, and you request access on its WhatsApp page.

Visit Tadlo AI

2. WATI

Best for: Brands with a large support desk

WATI's mature team inbox suits electronics brands where setup and troubleshooting questions fill the day. WooCommerce has an official integration; Shopify is a $4.99 a month add-on.

Price: Growth ₹2,999, Pro ₹6,499 and Business ₹18,499 a month billed monthly, with savings of up to 10% on quarterly and up to about 25% on annual billing. Growth includes 3 users with no additional users; Pro and Business include 5, with extra users at ₹1,299 and ₹2,199 a month each. Template and service messages are charged on top, with a markup on Meta's rates. A free trial is available.

Strengths

  • Mature support inbox
  • WooCommerce integration

Limitations

  • Markup percentage not published
  • Shopify is an add-on

Visit WATI

3. Interakt

Best for: Shopify electronics stores

Interakt includes Shopify on every WhatsApp plan, a good base for order updates and campaigns.

Price: WhatsApp plans: Growth ₹2,799 and Advanced ₹3,799 a month plus taxes; Enterprise on request with no markup. The ₹999 Starter plan covers Instagram only. Interakt charges its own per-message rates, which sit above Meta's: marketing ₹0.970 / ₹0.958 / ₹0.949 and utility ₹0.160 / ₹0.150 / ₹0.140 on Starter, Growth and Advanced. 14-day free trial.

Strengths

  • Shopify on every WhatsApp plan
  • 14-day trial

Limitations

  • Markup below Enterprise

4. AiSensy

Best for: Launch and accessory campaigns

AiSensy suits brands that push launches and accessory offers by broadcast.

Paid plans were Basic ₹1,500 and Pro ₹3,200 a month according to AiSensy's blog on 17 February 2025. Calculated from AiSensy's published India rates (marketing ₹1.09, utility ₹0.145) against Meta's rates, its message charges are about 26% higher than Meta's.

Order updates matter more here

Gadget buyers track their parcel closely and contact support when updates stop. Shipped, out-for-delivery and delivered messages, sent as utility templates at ₹0.115 each to Indian numbers, cut those "where is my order" questions sharply and keep the buyer expecting the courier.

Warranty and setup

Put the setup guide and the warranty registration link in the delivered update, a utility template tied to the order. Keep it factual: what the warranty covers, how to register, and how to reach support. Avoid stacking a sales offer into the same message; save accessories for a separate, opted-in campaign.

Accessory upsells done right

Buyers of a phone or speaker often want a case, cable or stand. Offer accessories to opted-in buyers a few days after delivery, once they have used the product, and only accessories compatible with what they bought. Broadcasts with a cost preview help you keep these campaigns small and targeted.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best WhatsApp automation for an electronics store?

Tadlo AI is our top pick: order and delivery updates, COD confirmation, cart recovery and campaigns with cost preview, with no markup on Meta's charges.

Can I send warranty information on WhatsApp automatically?

Yes. Include the warranty terms and a registration link in the delivered update, which is a utility template tied to the order.

Should I upsell accessories on WhatsApp?

Yes, to opted-in buyers, a few days after delivery, and only with accessories that fit what they bought. Keep it to one message per purchase.

How do gadget brands reduce "where is my order" questions?

Send shipped, out-for-delivery and delivered updates on WhatsApp for every order.
