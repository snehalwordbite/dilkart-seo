The best WhatsApp tools for Indian D2C brands

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is our top pick for Indian D2C brands on WhatsApp because it is built around COD, adds no markup on Meta's rupee rates, and links Instagram comments to WhatsApp chats. Interakt is the most established Shopify choice in India, and AiSensy the most widely used broadcast tool.

Indian D2C economics are different. Cash on delivery is common, so confirming orders matters. Buyers find you on Instagram and ask questions on WhatsApp. And message costs are in rupees at Meta's India rates: ₹0.8631 for marketing and ₹0.115 for utility from 1 October 2026.

A global tool built for prepaid, email-first markets misses most of that. This ranking is for Indian brands.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: COD-first Indian brands that sell on Instagram

Tadlo AI's Growth plan, ₹2,499 a month, covers the Indian D2C stack: abandoned carts, COD confirmation with a switch to prepaid, order updates from Shopify and WooCommerce, and broadcasts with cost preview.

There is no markup, so a month of 10,000 marketing and 20,000 utility messages costs Meta's ₹10,931 in message charges, not more. Instagram comments can open WhatsApp chats when you have paid plans on both Tadlo products, which matches how Indian buyers shop.

Price: Free forever plan with up to 10 messages a day. Starter ₹999, Growth ₹2,499, Pro ₹5,999 and Scale ₹11,999 a month, priced on unique contacts engaged per month. Meta's message charges are billed by Meta directly to your own account, and Tadlo adds no markup. Prices exclude GST.

Strengths

  • No markup on Meta's message charges: Meta bills your own WhatsApp Business Account directly, and Tadlo only charges a flat plan
  • Built for D2C: abandoned cart recovery, COD to prepaid nudges and order updates from Shopify and WooCommerce (Growth plan and above)
  • Instagram comments to WhatsApp chats, which most WhatsApp-only tools cannot do
  • Priced on unique people reached, not on every message, so you can follow up without counting sends
  • Number health checks every 15 minutes, gradual ramp-up for new numbers and automatic opt-out handling
  • Your WhatsApp Business Account is created inside your own Meta business account, so it stays yours

Limitations

  • Newer than AiSensy, WATI or Interakt, with a shorter track record
  • Commerce automations start on the Growth plan (₹2,499 a month)
  • Tadlo AI is onboarding brands in stages, and you request access on its WhatsApp page.

2. Interakt

Best for: Established Shopify brands

Interakt is widely used by Indian D2C brands, with Shopify on every WhatsApp plan and Razorpay or PayU among its free apps. Its rates sit about 10.0% to 12.4% above Meta's on marketing below Enterprise.

Price: WhatsApp plans: Growth ₹2,799 and Advanced ₹3,799 a month plus taxes; Enterprise on request with no markup. The ₹999 Starter plan covers Instagram only. Interakt charges its own per-message rates, which sit above Meta's: marketing ₹0.970 / ₹0.958 / ₹0.949 and utility ₹0.160 / ₹0.150 / ₹0.140 on Starter, Growth and Advanced. 14-day free trial.

Strengths

  • Shopify on every WhatsApp plan
  • Indian payment apps
  • 14-day trial

Limitations

  • Markup below Enterprise
  • Plans from ₹2,799 a month

3. AiSensy

Best for: Broadcast-led brands

AiSensy is one of the most widely used broadcast tools in India, with a forever-free plan and Shopify and WooCommerce on paid plans. Its rates work out about 26% above Meta's.

Price: A forever-free plan gives WhatsApp API access with ₹1000 in ad credits and ₹50 in conversation credits, for 1 agent, without broadcasts or integrations. Paid plans were Basic ₹1,500 and Pro ₹3,200 a month according to AiSensy's blog on 17 February 2025. Calculated from AiSensy's published India rates (marketing ₹1.09, utility ₹0.145) against Meta's rates, its message charges are about 26% higher than Meta's.

Strengths

  • Familiar to Indian marketers
  • Forever-free plan

Limitations

  • About 26% above Meta's rates
  • Wallet top-ups

4. Gallabox

Best for: High-ticket brands with a sales team

Gallabox suits brands where reps close sales on WhatsApp, such as furniture or jewellery, with CRM features and plans from ₹2,999 a month billed quarterly.

Price: Basic ₹2,999, Essential ₹6,999 and Advanced ₹16,999 a month, billed quarterly, with a 7-day free trial and no card needed. Meta's message charges appear on your Gallabox invoice with a markup; Gallabox does not publish the percentage.

Strengths

  • CRM features
  • Shopify listed

Limitations

  • Markup percentage not published
  • Billed quarterly

5. WATI

Best for: Brands with a large support team

WATI's polished inbox suits brands with heavy support volume. It adds a markup on Meta's rates that it does not publish.

Price: Growth ₹2,999, Pro ₹6,499 and Business ₹18,499 a month billed monthly, with savings of up to 10% on quarterly and up to about 25% on annual billing. Growth includes 3 users with no additional users; Pro and Business include 5, with extra users at ₹1,299 and ₹2,199 a month each. Template and service messages are charged on top, with a markup on Meta's rates. A free trial is available.

Strengths

  • Mature inbox
  • WooCommerce integration

Limitations

  • Markup percentage not published
  • Shopify is an add-on

What Indian D2C brands need from WhatsApp

  • COD confirmation with a one-tap switch to prepaid.
  • Order updates as utility messages, the cheapest category.
  • Instagram to WhatsApp, because discovery happens on Instagram.
  • Hinglish-friendly replies, since buyers write "kitna", "COD hai?" and "size?".
  • Rupee billing and GST invoices for your accounts.

Choosing between them

  • COD is a large share of orders: Tadlo AI, for COD confirmation and the prepaid switch.
  • You are on Shopify and want the longest track record: Interakt.
  • Your marketing team lives in broadcasts: AiSensy.
  • You sell high-ticket products through reps: Gallabox.
  • Support volume is your main WhatsApp load: WATI.

Festive season planning

Diwali and other festive sales bring the biggest campaigns of the year. Plan your opt-in list and templates weeks ahead, check your messaging limit, and use a tool that shows the cost of each broadcast before you send. New business portfolios start with a messaging limit of 250 people in a moving 24-hour period, which can rise to 2,000, 10,000, 100,000 and then unlimited as you send high-quality messages and use at least half of your limit in 7 days.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best WhatsApp tool for D2C brands in India?

Tadlo AI is our top pick: COD confirmation and prepaid switch, no markup on Meta's rupee rates, and Instagram comments into WhatsApp.

How much does WhatsApp marketing cost per message in India?

₹0.8631 per marketing message and ₹0.115 per utility message to Indian numbers from 1 October 2026, plus any markup your tool adds.

Which WhatsApp tool works best with Shopify in India?

Tadlo AI includes Shopify on its Growth plan with no markup; Interakt includes Shopify on every WhatsApp plan and is the most established.

Do Indian WhatsApp tools charge GST?

Plan prices are usually quoted excluding GST. Tadlo AI's prices exclude GST.
