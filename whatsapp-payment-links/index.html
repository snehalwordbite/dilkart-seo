Payment links on WhatsApp: getting paid in the chat

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Send a payment link in the WhatsApp chat at the moment the buyer is ready: after a question is answered, at COD confirmation, or when a cart is left. Then track which chats produced paid orders. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job; its Pro plan includes payment links in chat and revenue attribution.

Every step between "I'll take it" and "paid" loses buyers. A payment link inside the WhatsApp chat removes most of those steps: no browsing back to the site, no rebuilding the cart.

This guide covers where payment links fit, how to send them safely, and how to see the revenue they bring.

Where payment links fit

Moment and message
MomentWhat to send
Buyer asks questions, then says yesA link for exactly that product and size
COD order confirmedOption to pay now instead, with a link
Custom or made-to-order itemA link for the agreed price and deposit
Cart left with a questionAnswer, then a link to complete payment

Make the link trustworthy

  • Use a recognised payment gateway and say its name in the message.
  • Show the amount and what it is for in the same message.
  • Make the link open on a phone without logging in.
  • Confirm payment in the same chat right away.
  • Never ask buyers to send card numbers or bank details in chat. WhatsApp's Business Messaging Policy restricts health information and financial account details, and lists medical products as a regulated vertical.

COD to prepaid with a link

The highest-value use is at COD confirmation: "Confirm, cancel, or pay now". A buyer who taps Pay now gets the link in the same chat and the order becomes prepaid, one that cannot be refused at the door. See our guide to converting COD to prepaid for how to size any incentive.

Tracking revenue from chats

Without tracking, WhatsApp looks like a cost centre. With it, you can see which conversations, campaigns and automations produced paid orders. Use a tool with revenue attribution, or tag each payment link with the conversation or campaign it came from.

Setting it up

  1. Connect your payment gateway or store checkout to your WhatsApp tool.
  2. Decide who can send links: automations, agents, or both.
  3. Create a template for payment requests sent outside the customer service window.
  4. Test with a real small payment and check the order updates in your store.
  5. Review payment-link revenue weekly for the first month.

A sample payment message

Payment link

Hi {{first_name}}, here is the payment link for {{product}} ({{size}}): {{payment_link}}
Amount: {{amount}}, paid securely through {{gateway}}.
We will confirm here as soon as it is paid.

Deposits for custom orders

For made-to-order or personalised products, a payment link for a deposit confirms the order and protects you from cancellations after work has started. State the deposit, the balance and when the balance is due in the same message.

When not to send a link

  • Before the buyer has said they want to buy; it reads as pressure.
  • To someone who asked a support question about a past order.
  • More than once in a day for the same item.

Costs

Links sent inside a conversation the buyer started are service replies; Meta gives 1,000 free service messages a month per number. Payment requests sent as templates outside that window are charged by category. Gateway fees apply to each payment as usual.

Frequently asked questions

Can I collect payments on WhatsApp?

Yes, by sending a payment link in the chat that opens your payment gateway. Tadlo AI includes payment links in chat on its Pro plan.

Are WhatsApp payment links safe?

They are as safe as the gateway behind them. Use a recognised gateway, show the amount, and never ask for card or bank details in chat.

How do I track revenue from WhatsApp?

Use a tool with revenue attribution, such as Tadlo AI's Pro plan, or tag each link with the campaign or conversation it came from.
