How to answer "price?" on Instagram automatically

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Create a keyword rule for "price" and every variant your buyers use, and reply in the DM with the product, the price and a buy link within seconds. Add a short public reply so others see you answered. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job: keyword rules on comments and DMs, product carousels and unlimited DMs.

"Price?" might be the most common comment on Indian product reels. Each one is a buyer with intent, and each one unanswered for an hour is likely gone.

Answering them by hand does not scale past a few posts a week. This guide sets up an automatic reply that is fast, accurate and still feels personal.

Cover every way people ask

List the variants from your own comments. Common ones: price, prize, rate, cost, kitna, kitne ka, "how much", "details", "dm", "pp" and an emoji or two. Add them all to one rule. Review the comments weekly and add new ones; buyers are inventive.

What the reply should contain

  1. The product name and price, in the first line.
  2. One button to the product page.
  3. Delivery time and whether COD is available.
  4. A way to ask more: a WhatsApp button or "reply here".

Should you put the price in the caption?

Some brands hide prices to drive DMs. It can lift comment counts, but it frustrates buyers who just wanted the number. A middle path: show a starting price in the caption, and let the DM send the exact price for each size or variant, with the link.

Comments and DMs

Some buyers comment, others DM you "price" directly, and others reply to a story. Use the same keyword rule across all three so the answer is identical wherever they ask.

Public replies

Reply under the comment with a short line such as "Sent you the price". Rotate a few versions; identical replies hundreds of times look automated and can be filtered as spam.

Multiple products in one post

For a reel with several products, send a carousel instead of one link, so the buyer sees every price at once and does not need to ask again for the second item.

Keep prices current

An automatic price reply is only as good as the price in it. When you run a sale or change a price, update the DM on the same day, or link to the product page and let the page show the live price. Old prices in automated replies are one of the quickest ways to lose a buyer's trust.

A note on stories and Lives

Price questions also arrive as story replies and during Lives. Use the same keyword rule for story replies, and on Lives, ask a team member to pin a comment with the price and keyword so the chat does not fill with the same question.

When to hand over to a person

  • Bulk or wholesale enquiries.
  • Custom sizes or made-to-order requests.
  • Questions the automation cannot answer, such as stock in a specific city.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reply to price comments on Instagram automatically?

Set a comment-to-DM rule for "price" and its variants that sends the product, price and buy link, with a short public reply.

What keywords should I use for price enquiries?

Price, prize, rate, cost, kitna, kitne ka and "how much", plus any others you see in your own comments.

Can I answer price questions in Hinglish?

Yes. Match the keywords in Hinglish, and write the reply in the language your buyers use most; many brands keep the price line in English and the greeting in Hinglish.

What if the product has many sizes at different prices?

Send the price range in the DM with a link to the product page, where the buyer picks the size and sees the exact price, or send a carousel with one card per variant.

How fast should the price reply arrive?

Within seconds. Tadlo says its comment-to-DM replies typically go out within 2 to 5 seconds, while the buyer is still on the post.

Should I show prices in my Instagram captions?

Showing a starting price helps buyers decide to ask. Use the DM for exact prices per size or variant, with a link.
