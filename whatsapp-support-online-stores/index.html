WhatsApp customer support for online stores

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Run WhatsApp support from a shared inbox, let automation answer "where is my order" and other repeat questions, and route returns and complaints to a person quickly. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job; it includes a shared team inbox from its Starter plan and sends order updates that prevent most status questions.

WhatsApp is where Indian buyers expect to reach a store. That makes it the busiest support channel for most D2C brands, and the one where slow replies cost the most goodwill.

This guide sets up support that answers quickly without hiring a large team.

Prevent the most common question

"Where is my order?" is usually the largest share of support messages. Order confirmations and shipping updates sent automatically answer it before it is asked. Switch those on first and the inbox gets noticeably quieter.

Set up a shared inbox

  • One WhatsApp number, several team members, each conversation assigned to one person.
  • Notes on each conversation so whoever picks it up knows the history.
  • Tags for order status, returns, product questions and complaints.

Returns and exchanges

  1. Confirm the order and the item.
  2. Ask for a photo if the item is damaged or wrong.
  3. Explain the next step and the timeline in one message.
  4. Send a confirmation when the pickup or exchange is booked.
  5. Close the loop when the refund or replacement is done.

Response times

Set business hours and an away message that says when you will reply. During hours, aim to answer within minutes, not hours. Replies inside the customer service window are service messages, and the first 1,000 each month per number are free.

When to call instead

  • An angry customer after two messages.
  • A high-value order with a delivery problem.
  • Anything that needs several back-and-forth questions.

Complaints in public

When a buyer complains in Instagram comments, reply publicly with a short, polite note and move the conversation to WhatsApp. Never automate replies to complaints.

Automation that helps, not hides

Use automation for answers that do not change: order status, return policy, delivery times. Do not use it to keep buyers away from a person. A clear "talk to our team" option in every automated reply keeps trust high, especially when something has gone wrong.

Staffing

Seats decide how many people can work the inbox at once. Plan for your busiest day, such as the day after a festive campaign, not your average day.

Frequently asked questions

How do online stores do customer support on WhatsApp?

With a shared inbox for the team, automated order updates to prevent status questions, and quick replies for returns, sizes and delivery.

Should returns be handled on WhatsApp or by email?

WhatsApp is faster for buyers and easier for photos of damaged items. Keep a written confirmation of the return in the chat so both sides have a record.

How fast should a store reply on WhatsApp?

Within minutes during business hours. Buyers on WhatsApp expect a conversation, and a slow first reply often means a lost sale or a frustrated customer.

What should a WhatsApp away message say?

When you will reply, where to track an order in the meantime, and that the message has been received. Keep it to two or three lines.

Can several people answer the same WhatsApp number?

Yes, through a shared inbox on the WhatsApp Business Platform. Tadlo AI includes one from its Starter plan.

Are WhatsApp support replies free?

Replies to customers who messaged you are service messages; Meta gives each number 1,000 free a month and charges after that.
