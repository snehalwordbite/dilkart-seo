Back-in-stock alerts on WhatsApp: waitlists and restock broadcasts

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Collect a waitlist per product with WhatsApp opt-in, then send a restock broadcast to that list when stock lands. We recommend Tadlo AI for sending it: broadcasts show the cost before you send and leave opted-out customers out. Restock messages go out as a broadcast you send, not an automatic trigger.

A sold-out product is a list of people who already said yes. When it comes back, the fastest way to turn them into orders is a WhatsApp message, because it gets read within minutes and the buyer can tap straight through.

This guide covers building the waitlist, sending the restock message, and doing it at the right cost. With Tadlo AI, restock, winback and review messages are sent as opted-in broadcasts from the Growth plan, with the cost shown before you send. We did not find scheduled or event-triggered restock, winback, review or reorder automations in its published plans, so you choose the segment and send each broadcast yourself.

Building the waitlist

  1. Add a "Notify me on WhatsApp" button on sold-out product pages.
  2. Collect the phone number with clear opt-in wording that names your store and the product.
  3. Tag each contact with the product and size they want.
  4. On Instagram, a RESTOCK keyword can collect interest and send people to the same opt-in.
  5. Keep the list per product, not one big list.

Opt-in wording

"Message me on WhatsApp when this is back in stock. [Store] may also send offers; reply STOP to opt out." Meta requires businesses to collect opt-in that clearly states the person is agreeing to receive messages from the business, and names the business. If you only promise a restock alert, send only the restock alert.

Sending the restock broadcast

  1. When stock arrives, update the product page first.
  2. Select the segment: people waiting for that product and size.
  3. Check the cost preview before sending.
  4. Send the approved template with the product link.
  5. Watch replies; some will ask about other sizes.

Template category and cost

A restock alert is promotional, so Meta classes it as marketing: ₹0.8631 per message to Indian numbers from 1 October 2026. A list of 500 waiting buyers costs about the same as a small ad, and it reaches people who already asked for the product.

Stock smaller than the list?

If you restock 50 units and 500 people are waiting, say so honestly: "Limited units back today." Consider sending in batches, starting with the earliest sign-ups, so you do not disappoint hundreds of people in one go.

Timing

  • Send when the product is live and checkout works.
  • Avoid late night; morning or early evening gets more reads.
  • Do not send a second restock message the same day.

After the restock

Remove people who bought from the waiting segment, so they never get a second alert for something they already own. Keep the others for the next restock, or let the list expire after a set time and say so in the opt-in.

Restock alerts from Instagram

Sold-out products get "restock?" comments on Instagram. Reply with a DM that invites them to the WhatsApp waitlist, since Instagram limits messages to 24 hours after their last message and cannot reach them on restock day.

Measure

Track waitlist sign-ups per product, broadcast reads, clicks and orders. Products with long waitlists tell you what to reorder in larger quantities.
