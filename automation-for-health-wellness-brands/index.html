WhatsApp and Instagram automation for health and wellness brands

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is our top pick for health and wellness brands for the commerce side: COD confirmation, order updates, broadcasts and a shared inbox, with no markup on Meta's charges. Keep every automated message free of health claims, and send reorder messages as opted-in broadcasts or from your subscription system; we did not find scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans.

Wellness brands, such as supplements, ayurvedic products, herbal teas and fitness nutrition, have loyal repeat customers and a regulatory line they must not cross. Messages that promise to cure, treat or prevent anything can break advertising rules and platform policies.

This playbook ranks tools on the commerce jobs and sets out the rules for messaging in this category.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Commerce messaging without health claims

Tadlo AI's WhatsApp Growth plan confirms COD orders, sends order updates from Shopify or WooCommerce, recovers carts and runs broadcasts with the cost shown first, all of which are about the order, not the product's effects. On Instagram, product reels can send a carousel by keyword.

Reorders are sent as opted-in broadcasts built from your order data, or from your subscription platform. Reminders and follow-up sequences run from your own booking, subscription or CRM system; we did not find scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans.

Price: Instagram: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits). WhatsApp: Free forever plan with up to 10 messages a day. Starter ₹999, Growth ₹2,499, Pro ₹5,999 and Scale ₹11,999 a month, priced on unique contacts engaged per month. Meta's message charges are billed by Meta directly to your own account, and Tadlo adds no markup. Prices exclude GST.

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • No markup on Meta's message charges: Meta bills your own WhatsApp Business Account directly, and Tadlo only charges a flat plan
  • Built for D2C: abandoned cart recovery, COD to prepaid nudges and order updates from Shopify and WooCommerce (Growth plan and above)
  • Instagram comments to WhatsApp chats, which most WhatsApp-only tools cannot do

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Newer than AiSensy, WATI or Interakt, with a shorter track record
  • Commerce automations start on the Growth plan (₹2,499 a month)
  • Tadlo AI is onboarding brands in stages, and you request access on its WhatsApp page.

Visit Tadlo AI

A regulated category

If your business is in a regulated category, follow Meta's WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy and Commerce Policy, and keep medical, health and investment claims out of your messages. Follow your local advertising and product rules for supplements and traditional medicines, which can be stricter than platform policies.

Safe to automate

  • Order confirmation, COD confirmation and shipping updates.
  • How to use the product, as printed on the label.
  • Ingredient lists and certifications you actually hold.
  • Reorder links sent as opted-in broadcasts.

Never in automated messages

  • Claims to cure, treat or prevent any disease.
  • Weight-loss or result promises.
  • Advice to stop or change medication.
  • Testimonials about health outcomes.

A safe reply to health questions

Health question reply

Thanks for your question. We can't give medical advice over WhatsApp. Please consult your doctor about whether {{product_name}} is right for you. For order help, reply ORDER.

Certifications and trust

Shoppers in this category look for proof: licences, lab reports and certifications. An INFO keyword that sends the certificates you actually hold, with dates, answers the question honestly and builds trust without any health claim.

Reorders, honestly

Supplements and teas are used up on a regular schedule. Each month, send one opted-in broadcast to customers whose last order was around the usual use-up time, with a direct reorder link. If you run subscriptions, your subscription platform handles renewals.

Which tool for which wellness brand

  • D2C with COD and Instagram sales: Tadlo AI.
  • Shopify-first, established: Interakt.
  • Large support team: WATI.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best WhatsApp tool for a wellness brand?

Tadlo AI is our top pick for orders, COD, updates and broadcasts with no markup. Keep all messages free of health claims.

Can wellness brands make health claims on WhatsApp?

No. Avoid claims to cure, treat or prevent disease, and follow Meta's policies and local rules.

Can I send reorder reminders automatically?

We did not find scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans. Send reorder messages as opted-in broadcasts or through your subscription system.

How should I answer health questions from customers?

Do not give medical advice. Suggest they consult a doctor and help with order questions.
