Instagram automation for D2C brands: from comment to checkout

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Turn comments into checkouts in four steps: a keyword on every product post, a DM with the product and price, a capture step for email or WhatsApp, and a handoff for questions. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job: product carousels, unlimited DMs on every plan and Instagram-to-WhatsApp chats.

For a D2C brand, Instagram is the shop window and the comments are the queue at the counter. Every "price?" is a buyer who will leave if nobody answers.

This guide sets up the path from a comment to a checkout, with the decisions that matter at each step.

Step 1: put a keyword on every product post

Choose short, obvious keywords and say them out loud in the reel: PRICE, LINK, SHOP. Add the spelling variants your buyers actually type, including "prize", "rate", "kitna" and "details". One post can carry several rules: PRICE for the product, SIZE for the size chart, CODE for a discount.

Step 2: the first DM does the selling

  • Lead with the product and price, not "Hi, thanks for your interest".
  • Show the product: a single card, or a carousel of every piece in the post.
  • One button to the product page.
  • One line on delivery and COD, because that is the next question.

Step 3: capture the buyer who is not ready

Most commenters will not buy today. Ask for something that lets you reach them later: an email for the launch list, or a WhatsApp opt-in for restock alerts. Instagram only lets you message freely within 24 hours of their last message, so a contact you own is worth more than a DM thread.

Step 4: hand serious buyers to WhatsApp

Buyers ask about COD, delivery and returns before paying, and they prefer WhatsApp for that. Add a button or keyword that opens a WhatsApp chat with your store, and answer there with a person or your WhatsApp automations.

Follow gates: use them carefully

A follow gate asks for a follow before sending the link. It grows your account, but every extra step loses some buyers. Use it for freebies and giveaways, not for product links; a buyer who wanted the price should get the price.

Public replies: short and varied

When the DM goes out, reply publicly too, so other viewers see that comments get answered. Keep it short and rotate a few versions, such as "Sent you the details", "Check your DMs" and "Just messaged you". Identical replies hundreds of times make the thread look automated and can draw spam filters.

A week-one checklist

  1. Add a keyword rule to your three best-selling product posts.
  2. Write the first DM with product, price, one button and a delivery note.
  3. Add a capture step for email or WhatsApp opt-in.
  4. Test from a second account, then switch on.
  5. After three days, read the comments the rules missed and add those words.

Stories as a second trigger

Buyers also ask in stories. A story with a question sticker or a keyword such as "PRICE" opens a DM conversation too, and story replies are on every Tadlo AI plan. Use stories for restocks and limited offers, where urgency matters.

Influencer posts

When a creator posts your product, give them their own keyword and discount code. Comments on their post trigger your DM, and you can compare creators by DMs, clicks and code use.
