Instagram DM reply templates for online stores

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Below are 12 Instagram DM reply templates for online stores, covering the questions buyers ask most, with variables like {{name}} and {{product_link}}. Use them as automated replies to comment keywords or as saved replies for your team. We recommend Tadlo AI for automating them, with keyword rules on comments and DMs and unlimited DMs on every plan.

Most Instagram DMs to a store are the same dozen questions. Answering them the same good way every time is faster for you and better for the buyer.

Each template below starts with the answer, then gives one next step. Replace the variables with your tool's field names or your own text.

Product questions

Template 1: price

Hi {{name}}! {{product_name}} is {{price}}. You can order it here: {{product_link}}

Template 2: several products

Hi {{name}}, here are all the pieces from that post with prices. Tap any to see more: {{carousel}}

Template 3: size

Hi {{name}}, here's our size chart for {{product_name}}: {{size_chart_link}}. It runs {{fit_note}}. Tell us your usual size if you'd like help choosing.

Template 4: in stock

Hi {{name}}, yes, {{product_name}} is in stock in {{available_sizes}}. Order here: {{product_link}}

Template 5: sold out

Hi {{name}}, {{product_name}} is sold out right now. Want us to tell you when it's back? Reply YES and we'll let you know.

Buying questions

Template 6: delivery time

Hi {{name}}, we usually deliver to {{city}} in {{delivery_days}} days after dispatch. Order here: {{product_link}}

Template 7: cash on delivery

Hi {{name}}, yes, cash on delivery is available for {{product_name}}. Just choose COD at checkout: {{product_link}}

Template 8: discount

Hi {{name}}, use code {{code}} for {{discount}} off, valid until {{expiry}}. Shop here: {{product_link}}

Template 9: returns

Hi {{name}}, you can return or exchange within {{return_days}} days of delivery. Full policy: {{policy_link}}

Everything else

Template 10: talk on WhatsApp

Hi {{name}}, happy to help with that. It's quickest on WhatsApp: {{whatsapp_link}}

Template 11: collab enquiry

Hi {{name}}, thanks for your interest in working with us! Please send your media kit to {{email}} and our team will reply within {{reply_days}} days.

Template 12: complaint

Hi {{name}}, we're sorry about this. Please share your order number and a photo, and someone from our team will personally sort it out today.

Template 13: bonus, bulk order

Hi {{name}}, thanks for asking about a bulk order. Tell us the product, quantity and delivery city, and our team will send a quote within {{reply_days}} days.

Automate or save as quick replies?

Templates 1 to 8 work well as automated replies to keywords such as PRICE, SIZE, STOCK and COD. Templates 9 to 12 are better as saved replies your team sends, because returns, collabs and complaints need a person's judgement. Never automate the complaint reply; use it as a starting point for a human answer.

Keep them current

  • Update prices and codes the day they change.
  • Check product links still work after you edit products.
  • Review sold-out replies when stock comes back.
