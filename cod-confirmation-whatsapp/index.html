COD confirmation on WhatsApp: designing the flow

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Send a utility message within minutes of every COD order showing the order and address, with three buttons: Confirm, Cancel, Pay now. Hold unconfirmed orders briefly, remind once, then follow your policy. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job; its Growth plan runs this flow from Shopify and WooCommerce orders.

COD confirmation is the highest-value message most Indian stores can send. It costs one utility message and can stop a parcel that would have come back.

The flow has more decisions than it looks. This guide walks through each one.

The message

COD confirmation, part by part
PartContent
HeaderYour store name and "Confirm your order"
OrderProducts, quantity, total to pay on delivery
AddressFull delivery address, so mistakes are caught
ButtonsConfirm, Cancel, Pay now instead

What happens after each button

  1. Confirm: mark the order confirmed in your store and release it for packing.
  2. Cancel: cancel the order, restock the item and thank them; a short survey of why can help later.
  3. Pay now: send a payment link in the same chat; once paid, convert the order to prepaid.
  4. Wrong address: let them reply with the correction and have a person update it the same day.

When there is no reply

Some buyers never reply. Decide your rule in advance: one reminder a few hours later, then either ship as normal or hold, depending on order value and your experience with similar orders. High-value or first-time orders are usually worth holding a little longer.

Timing

Send the confirmation within minutes of the order, while the buyer is still thinking about it. Messages sent the next day get fewer replies and more "what order?".

Cost

COD confirmations are utility messages: ₹0.115 each to Indian numbers from 1 October 2026. A reminder doubles that for the buyers who do not reply. Against the cost of shipping an order out and back, it is small.

Who should see the replies

Most replies are button taps that update the order on their own. The rest, address corrections, questions about delivery dates, requests to change the product, need a person. Route those to a shared inbox that someone checks several times a day, because an order held for an answer that never comes is a sale lost.

Handling cancellations well

A cancellation at confirmation is good news: it is a return you did not pay for. Make cancelling easy and polite, restock the item at once, and consider one optional question about why. Over a few months, the answers show patterns, such as buyers who expected faster delivery or a different size, that you can fix on the product page.

Common mistakes

  • Sending it the next day. Replies drop sharply once the buyer has moved on.
  • Asking them to call you. Buttons get answers; phone numbers do not.
  • Not updating the store. If a cancelled order still ships, the message did nothing.
  • Promotional wording. An offer in the confirmation can push it into the marketing category.
  • No plan for silence. Decide your no-reply rule before you switch the flow on.

Template approval tips

  • Keep it transactional: no offers or promotional language, or Meta may class it as marketing.
  • Use variables for the customer's name, order number, items, total and address.
  • Include the buttons in the template so replies are one tap.

Frequently asked questions

How do I confirm COD orders on WhatsApp?

Send a utility template right after each COD order with the order, address and buttons to confirm, cancel or pay now, then update the order based on the reply.

What should I do if the customer does not reply?

Send one reminder a few hours later, then follow your own rule: ship, or hold high-value and first-time orders a little longer.

Can I hold an order until the customer confirms?

Yes, that is your policy to set. Many stores hold high-value and first-time COD orders until they are confirmed, and ship the rest after one reminder.

Is a COD confirmation message marketing or utility?

Utility, as long as it is transactional with no promotion. It costs ₹0.115 to an Indian number from 1 October 2026.
