WhatsApp abandoned cart recovery: how to set it up

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Collect WhatsApp opt-in at checkout, get one cart reminder template approved, send it a few hours after the cart is left, and stop as soon as the order is placed. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job, because cart recovery comes on its Growth plan with COD confirmation and order updates, at Meta's rates with no markup.

Cart recovery on WhatsApp is simple in principle and easy to get wrong. Send too early and it feels like surveillance; send too late and the buyer has moved on; forget to stop and you remind people who already paid.

This guide covers the setup in order, then the message itself.

What you need before you start

  • Your store connected to a WhatsApp provider that receives cart events (Shopify or WooCommerce).
  • A checkout that collects a mobile number with country code early, before payment.
  • A WhatsApp opt-in at checkout with your brand name in the wording.
  • An approved marketing template for the reminder, with the product and a link back to the cart.

Setup, step by step

  1. Connect your store and confirm cart events are arriving in the WhatsApp tool.
  2. Write and submit the reminder template; marketing category, with a button to the cart.
  3. Set the first delay; a few hours is a common starting point.
  4. Turn on the stop rule so placed orders never receive a reminder.
  5. Decide on one follow-up, a day later, or none.
  6. Test with a real cart from a phone that opted in.

Discounts: hold them back

A discount in the first reminder teaches buyers to abandon carts on purpose. If you use one, put it in the follow-up only, keep it small, and give it an end date.

What it costs

Each reminder is a marketing message: ₹0.8631 to an Indian number from 1 October 2026. 5,000 reminders cost about ₹4,316 in Meta's charges. Compare that with the revenue recovered, not with the number of carts reminded.

Which carts to remind

Not every abandoned cart deserves a message. Skip carts where the buyer has already started a conversation with your team, carts from numbers that opted out, and very low-value carts where the message costs more than it is likely to recover. Some stores also skip carts abandoned within minutes of a placed order, since those are often duplicates.

The follow-up message

If you send a follow-up, change the angle rather than repeating the first message. Answer a likely doubt, such as delivery time or returns, or mention low stock if it is true. Send it a day after the first reminder, and only if the order has not been placed. Never send a third.

Common mistakes

  • Reminding buyers who already ordered, because the stop rule was not connected.
  • Linking to the home page instead of the cart.
  • Sending to numbers that never opted in.
  • Using the same template as campaigns, which trains buyers to ignore both.

Measure recovered revenue

Track carts reminded, orders placed within a few days of a reminder, and the revenue from those orders. Tools with revenue attribution, such as Tadlo AI's Pro plan, report this; otherwise tag the cart link with UTM parameters.

Frequently asked questions

How do I set up abandoned cart recovery on WhatsApp?

Connect your store to a WhatsApp provider, collect opt-in at checkout, get a reminder template approved, set a delay and a stop-on-purchase rule, then test.

How long should I wait before sending a cart reminder?

A few hours is a common starting point. Test on your own store, and see our guide to cart recovery timing.

Should I offer a discount in the cart reminder?

Not in the first one. If you use a discount, put it in a single follow-up with an end date.
