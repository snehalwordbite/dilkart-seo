How to reduce RTO, WhatsApp first

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Reduce RTO by confirming every COD order on WhatsApp, fixing addresses before dispatch, nudging to prepaid, and keeping buyers updated until delivery, then fix couriers and product pages. We recommend Tadlo AI for the WhatsApp side; its Growth plan covers confirmation, prepaid switch and delivery updates.

RTO is rarely one problem. It is a mix of buyers who changed their mind, addresses that were never going to work, couriers that missed them, and products that did not match the photos.

This guide lists every lever in the order we would pull them, with WhatsApp first because it is the fastest to switch on.

Lever 1: confirm before you ship

A COD confirmation right after the order catches changed minds and fake orders while they are cheap to cancel. Show the order and address, with Confirm, Cancel and Pay now buttons.

Lever 2: fix the address

Show the full address in the confirmation and invite corrections. Missing flat numbers, landmarks and pin codes cause failed deliveries that look like refusals in your data.

Lever 3: offer prepaid

Offer a switch to prepaid in the same message, with a payment link. Size any incentive with care: see our guide to converting COD to prepaid.

Lever 4: keep them expecting the parcel

Shipped and out-for-delivery updates make it more likely someone is home with cash or a payment app. They are utility messages, ₹0.115 each to Indian numbers.

Lever 5: courier and pin code

Look at RTO by courier and by pin code. Some routes fail far more often; switching courier or restricting COD on those pin codes may do more than any message.

Lever 6: product pages and expectations

Refusals at the door often come from a mismatch between the listing and the buyer's expectation: size, colour, quality. Better photos, size charts and honest descriptions reduce them.

Which orders to watch most closely

  • First-time buyers, who have no history with your store.
  • High-value COD orders, where a return costs the most.
  • Orders to pin codes with a history of failed deliveries.
  • Several COD orders from the same number in a short time.

These are the orders worth holding until they are confirmed, and the ones where a prepaid nudge pays back most.

Your first week

  1. Switch on COD confirmation for every COD order.
  2. Add the address to the confirmation and a way to correct it.
  3. Turn on shipped and out-for-delivery updates.
  4. Pull last quarter's returns by courier, pin code and product.
  5. Pick the one non-WhatsApp fix with the biggest share of returns and start on it.

Talking to your courier

Couriers can often share delivery-attempt data: how many attempts were made, at what time, and the reason recorded. Ask for it. If many returns say "customer not available" but your buyers confirmed on WhatsApp, the problem may be the delivery attempt rather than the buyer.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reduce RTO for my online store?

Confirm COD orders before shipping, fix addresses, offer prepaid, send delivery updates, then review couriers, pin codes and product listings.

Can WhatsApp alone fix RTO?

No. It handles the buyer side: changed minds, wrong addresses and missed deliveries. Courier performance and product mismatch need other fixes.

Does a prepaid nudge annoy COD buyers?

Not if it is optional and easy to ignore. Offer it once, in the confirmation, with a clear Confirm button next to it, and never make the order depend on switching.

How long before I see RTO fall?

Confirmation works from the first order it touches. Give it a full month of orders, then compare COD return rates with the month before, keeping courier and product mix as similar as you can.

Which RTO lever should I start with?

COD confirmation on WhatsApp, because it is quick to set up and catches problems before the parcel leaves.
