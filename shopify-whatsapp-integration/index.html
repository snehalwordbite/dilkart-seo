How to connect Shopify to WhatsApp, step by step

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Connect Shopify to a WhatsApp Business Platform provider, approve four utility templates and a cart reminder, add opt-in at checkout, then switch on order updates, COD confirmation and cart recovery in that order. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job; Shopify order events, COD confirmation and cart recovery are included on its Growth plan with no markup.

Shopify does not send WhatsApp messages on its own. You connect it to a provider on Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, which listens to your store's events and sends the right template for each one.

This guide takes a Shopify store from nothing to its first live automations in an afternoon, plus a day or two for template approvals.

What you need

  • A Shopify store with checkout collecting phone numbers with country codes.
  • A Meta business account, verified if possible.
  • A phone number for WhatsApp that is not in use on the WhatsApp app, or one you are ready to move.
  • An account with a WhatsApp provider that supports Shopify.

Step 1: connect your WhatsApp number

Inside your provider, start Meta's embedded signup. Choose or create your Meta business account, add the number, verify it by code, and set your display name. Prefer a provider that creates the WhatsApp Business Account inside your own Meta business account, so the number stays yours if you switch later.

Step 2: connect Shopify

Install the provider's Shopify app or connect through its integration page, and approve the permissions it asks for: orders, customers and checkouts. Place a test order and check that the provider shows the event arriving.

Step 3: get your templates approved

Templates to submit first
TemplateCategoryVariables
Order confirmationUtilityName, order number, items, total
COD confirmationUtilityName, order number, total, address, buttons
ShippedUtilityName, order number, courier, tracking link
DeliveredUtilityName, order number
Cart reminderMarketingName, product, cart link

Step 4: add opt-in at checkout

Shopify's checkout can collect marketing consent for SMS and email; add clear WhatsApp wording through your provider's checkout option or a checkbox app, naming your store. Keep a record of each opt-in.

Step 5: switch on order updates

Map order created to the confirmation, fulfilment created to shipped, and delivered to the delivered message. Test each with a real order.

Step 6: switch on COD confirmation

For COD orders, replace the plain confirmation with the confirm, cancel or pay-now message, and decide what happens to unconfirmed orders.

Step 7: switch on cart recovery

Set the first delay, connect the stop rule so placed orders never get a reminder, and decide on one follow-up or none.

After launch: the first week

  • Read replies daily; they show what buyers still need to know.
  • Check template quality ratings after the first few hundred sends.
  • Count "where is my order" support messages against the week before.

Testing checklist

  • A prepaid order receives the confirmation, shipped and delivered messages.
  • A COD order receives the confirm, cancel or pay-now message.
  • An abandoned cart receives one reminder, and none after ordering.
  • Names, totals and links fill correctly on a phone.

Frequently asked questions

How do I integrate WhatsApp with Shopify?

Connect your store to a WhatsApp Business Platform provider that supports Shopify, get templates approved, add opt-in at checkout, and map store events to messages.

Does Shopify send WhatsApp messages by itself?

No. You need a provider on Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform connected to your store.

How long does Shopify WhatsApp setup take?

An afternoon for the connection and settings, plus a day or two for Meta to approve templates.
