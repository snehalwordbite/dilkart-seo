The best WhatsApp tools for abandoned cart recovery

By DilKart Editorial. Updated 16 October 2026.

Short answer

Tadlo AI is our top pick for WhatsApp abandoned cart recovery because cart recovery is included on its Growth plan alongside COD to prepaid and order updates, and Meta's charges are billed at cost with no markup. Interakt is the established Shopify alternative, and AiSensy suits stores that pair cart reminders with heavy campaigns.

A cart reminder on WhatsApp works because it reaches the buyer where they read messages. It also costs money: cart reminders are marketing messages, ₹0.8631 each to Indian numbers from 1 October 2026, and some tools add a markup on top.

Before choosing a tool, know the three settings that decide whether cart recovery helps or annoys: how long it waits, whether it stops once the order is placed, and how many follow-ups it sends. Ask each vendor to show you these in a demo; not all of them publish the details.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Cart recovery in the same plan as COD and order updates

Tadlo AI includes abandoned cart recovery on its Growth plan, ₹2,499 a month, using Shopify and WooCommerce events. The same plan confirms COD orders and sends order updates, so one tool covers the buyer from cart to doorstep.

Its published page does not list its delay or follow-up settings in detail, so check them during setup. What it does publish: no markup on Meta's charges, automatic opt-out handling, and revenue attribution on Pro, which shows how many carts the reminders actually recovered.

Price: Free forever plan with up to 10 messages a day. Starter ₹999, Growth ₹2,499, Pro ₹5,999 and Scale ₹11,999 a month, priced on unique contacts engaged per month. Meta's message charges are billed by Meta directly to your own account, and Tadlo adds no markup. Prices exclude GST.

Strengths

  • No markup on Meta's message charges: Meta bills your own WhatsApp Business Account directly, and Tadlo only charges a flat plan
  • Built for D2C: abandoned cart recovery, COD to prepaid nudges and order updates from Shopify and WooCommerce (Growth plan and above)
  • Instagram comments to WhatsApp chats, which most WhatsApp-only tools cannot do
  • Priced on unique people reached, not on every message, so you can follow up without counting sends
  • Number health checks every 15 minutes, gradual ramp-up for new numbers and automatic opt-out handling
  • Your WhatsApp Business Account is created inside your own Meta business account, so it stays yours

Limitations

  • Newer than AiSensy, WATI or Interakt, with a shorter track record
  • Commerce automations start on the Growth plan (₹2,499 a month)
  • Tadlo AI is onboarding brands in stages, and you request access on its WhatsApp page.

2. Interakt

Best for: Shopify stores with established flows

Interakt's Shopify integration is included on every WhatsApp plan and is widely used by Indian brands for store messages. Check its cart reminder options in the trial; its per-message marketing rate is ₹0.958 on Growth.

Price: WhatsApp plans: Growth ₹2,799 and Advanced ₹3,799 a month plus taxes; Enterprise on request with no markup. The ₹999 Starter plan covers Instagram only. Interakt charges its own per-message rates, which sit above Meta's: marketing ₹0.970 / ₹0.958 / ₹0.949 and utility ₹0.160 / ₹0.150 / ₹0.140 on Starter, Growth and Advanced. 14-day free trial.

Strengths

  • Shopify on every WhatsApp plan
  • 14-day trial to test

Limitations

  • Markup on marketing messages below Enterprise
  • WhatsApp plans from ₹2,799

3. AiSensy

Best for: Cart reminders alongside heavy campaigns

AiSensy connects Shopify and WooCommerce on paid plans and is strong on retargeting campaigns. Its marketing rate of ₹1.09 per message to Indian numbers is about 26% above Meta's, which adds up on cart reminders.

Price: A forever-free plan gives WhatsApp API access with ₹1000 in ad credits and ₹50 in conversation credits, for 1 agent, without broadcasts or integrations. Paid plans were Basic ₹1,500 and Pro ₹3,200 a month according to AiSensy's blog on 17 February 2025. Calculated from AiSensy's published India rates (marketing ₹1.09, utility ₹0.145) against Meta's rates, its message charges are about 26% higher than Meta's.

Strengths

  • Retargeting strength
  • Shopify and WooCommerce on paid plans

Limitations

  • About 26% above Meta's rates
  • Wallet top-ups

4. WATI

Best for: Stores already using WATI for support

WATI connects Shopify through a $4.99 a month add-on and WooCommerce through an official integration. If your support team already uses WATI, adding cart reminders keeps everything in one inbox.

Price: Growth ₹2,999, Pro ₹6,499 and Business ₹18,499 a month billed monthly, with savings of up to 10% on quarterly and up to about 25% on annual billing. Growth includes 3 users with no additional users; Pro and Business include 5, with extra users at ₹1,299 and ₹2,199 a month each. Template and service messages are charged on top, with a markup on Meta's rates. A free trial is available.

Strengths

  • One inbox for support and cart replies
  • WooCommerce integration

Limitations

  • Markup percentage not published
  • Shopify is an add-on

The three settings that matter

What to check in any cart recovery tool
SettingWhy it mattersWhat we suggest
First delayToo soon feels pushy; too late and they have bought elsewhereOne to a few hours after the cart is left
Stop on purchaseReminding someone who already ordered loses trustMust stop the moment the order is placed
Follow-upsEach one is another marketing messageOne reminder, at most one follow-up a day later

What a cart reminder should say

  • The product name and photo, so they recognise it.
  • A one-tap link back to the same cart, not the home page.
  • A reason to finish: low stock, free delivery threshold, or COD available.
  • A way to ask a question, since many carts are abandoned over doubts about size or delivery.
  • No discount in the first message; save it for the follow-up if you use one at all.

What cart recovery costs

Each reminder is a marketing message. At ₹0.8631 to Indian numbers, 5,000 reminders a month cost Meta's charges of about ₹4,316, before any tool markup. Only message people who opted in at checkout, and keep follow-ups to one, so the cost stays proportionate to the carts you recover.

Measure what you recover

Track three numbers: carts reminded, carts recovered within a few days, and revenue from those orders. Tools with revenue attribution, such as Tadlo AI's Pro plan, do this for you; otherwise use UTM parameters on the cart link.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best WhatsApp abandoned cart tool?

Tadlo AI is our top pick: cart recovery on its Growth plan with COD to prepaid and order updates, and no markup on Meta's charges.

When should I send an abandoned cart WhatsApp message?

Most stores send the first reminder one to a few hours after the cart is left, and at most one follow-up a day later. Test timing on your own store.

Are abandoned cart WhatsApp messages marketing or utility?

Marketing. They cost ₹0.8631 each to Indian numbers from 1 October 2026.

Do I need permission to send cart reminders on WhatsApp?

Yes. Collect a clear WhatsApp opt-in at checkout before sending marketing messages.
